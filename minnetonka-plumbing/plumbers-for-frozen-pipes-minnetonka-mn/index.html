    Preventive Pipe Winterization in Minnetonka

    Winterizing before the first hard freeze is cheaper than one burst pipe. Every exposed line gets marked. We insulate attic lines, cover vents, upgrade outdoor faucets and drain irrigation lines.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Minnetonka, MN

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a hose bib with the hose left on. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow devices sit fully exposed, so we drain or cover them before winter and repair split ones.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Minnetonka

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.
