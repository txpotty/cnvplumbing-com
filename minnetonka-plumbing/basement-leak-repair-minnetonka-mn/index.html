    Supply Line Leak Repair in Minnetonka

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again.     Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Minnetonka

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water.     Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Minnetonka

    A floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. We cable or jet the line, then camera it to confirm. In homes where the street sewer backs up in storms, a backwater valve stops sewage from rising through the floor drain. Dry traps smell.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale from the inside out. We cut out cracked cast iron and splice in new pipe with shielded no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. A separated PVC joint usually means the pipe was not fully seated or not supported.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Minnetonka, MN

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one.     Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Plenty of basements rely on a sump pit to handle runoff and groundwater. Summer thunderstorms can drop intense rain in a short time, causing sewer backups in older neighborhoods.
