    Pipe Bursting Services in Minnetonka, MN

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Minnetonka, MN soil.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Minnetonka

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Minnetonka

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Minnetonka yard.
