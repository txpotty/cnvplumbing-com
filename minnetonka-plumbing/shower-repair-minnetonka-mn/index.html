    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Minnetonka

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us. CNV Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Minnetonka

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. If the valve body is pitted or parts no longer exist, we swap the valve, often through an access opening from the closet side. New valves include pressure balancing. You can step up to a thermostatic valve for tighter control.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Minnetonka

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Minnetonka, MN

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Minnetonka

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.
