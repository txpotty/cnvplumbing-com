    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Grease Trap Replacement and Upgrades in Minnetonka

    Plastic and steel traps both wear out, just in different ways. Steel rusts at the seams and the baffle welds. Plastic cracks. We remove the old unit, set the new one in the same spot when it fits, and use no-hub couplings so future work is easier.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Minnetonka, MN

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. We also check that a pump truck can reach an outdoor tank with about 100 feet of hose, since a tank the hauler cannot reach costs you more on every service.

    Grease Trap Brands We Install in Minnetonka

    Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura cover most of what we set in commercial kitchens, plus precast concrete tanks for the big outdoor interceptors. The lid gaskets and baffles differ from brand to brand. Parts availability matters. We lean toward units with parts a local supply house keeps on the shelf, so a cracked lid does not close you for a week.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Without it, a dumped sink pushes water through faster than the unit is rated for and the grease goes out the outlet. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Minnetonka

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Minnetonka, MN

    Price follows size and location more than brand. The low end is a small indoor unit with easy access and a short pipe run. The high end is an outdoor gravity interceptor with excavation, a traffic-rated lid and a permit, which can run five figures once concrete restoration is counted. We quote after a site visit.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Minnetonka

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties. From there we pick a hydromechanical unit or a gravity tank and figure out where it can sit with a straight shot to the building sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Minnetonka

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. The tank holds water for roughly half an hour, which gives grease time to rise and food solids time to drop. We size by fixture count and retention time, then match the tank to what the local pretreatment office for your building will accept. No moving parts to fail.
