    What Our Gas Leak Repairs Cover in Minnetonka, MN

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Minnetonka

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. The tag usually says what they found, so we start there. Then we test everything. Once the repair is done, we perform the required test and send the documentation to the utility or the inspector. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day or two.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. The first-stage regulator at the tank and the second-stage regulator at the house both get checked. Low spots get sniffed first. If the supplier owns the tank, they handle the tank side and we handle the piping from there to the appliances. Rural properties with underground tanks get a line test too.

    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Minnetonka

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. The first step is a pressure gauge on the piping, which tells us whether the system is actually losing gas. Then the sniffer comes out. A combustible gas detector follows the reading from fitting to fitting until it spikes. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final confirmation.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. Repairs use the same brand's fittings as the existing tubing, since manufacturers do not allow mixing parts. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Permit and Inspection Coordination in Minnetonka

    Replacing more than a connector usually means a permit, and in Minnetonka the inspector will want to see a gauge holding pressure before the pipe is covered. We pull the permit and book the inspection ourselves. Most inspections take under twenty minutes. The test is set to whatever the local fuel gas code requires, often a few psi held for fifteen minutes or longer. The signed permit card stays with your house.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time. Drywall stays intact when possible. Rerouting with CSST past a leak in a finished wall is often cheaper than cutting and patching drywall. Nothing is turned back on until the test gauge holds steady.

    Permitting and Code Compliance in Minnetonka, MN
