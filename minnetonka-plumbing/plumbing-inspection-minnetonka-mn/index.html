    Plumbing Inspection Work in Minnetonka, MN

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Minnetonka

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. We note anything above 80 psi.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Minnetonka, MN

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Minnetonka, MN

    Water heaters and boilers run long hours through the heating season, which adds wear compared to milder climates. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Minnetonka, MN

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Minnetonka, MN

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Minnetonka

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Minnetonka, MN

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Minnetonka, MN

    Older homes in Minnetonka often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. Then we locate it. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.
