    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Minnetonka, MN

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Minnetonka, MN

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Minnetonka

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. We handle all of it. A loop saves time.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Minnetonka

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Minnetonka, MN

    Building a home in Minnetonka, MN? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. We work with builders. Hose bibs can stay on hard water.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Minnetonka

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.
