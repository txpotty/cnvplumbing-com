    Commercial Water Line Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. No water means no open sign. CNV Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, and we bring the larger fittings and couplings on the truck. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. CNV Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. We can usually stop it within the hour. If the repair will run long, a temporary bypass keeps water flowing to the house during the repair.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Minnetonka

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older homes in Minnesota often still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, both of which corrode and clog with age. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Minnetonka

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Minnetonka, MN

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Minnetonka

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

The written price comes before we start.

    Yes, we stand behind it. A failed joint gets fixed at no charge. The workmanship warranty covers our labor on the repaired section, while the pipe and fittings carry their maker's warranty on top. All of it is listed on the service agreement you sign before we start.

    In most places the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner owns the line to the house.
