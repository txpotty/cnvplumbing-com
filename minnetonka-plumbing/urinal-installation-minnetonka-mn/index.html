    Commercial Urinal Installation in Minnetonka

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Minnetonka

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Minnetonka

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Minnetonka

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Minnetonka, MN

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Minnetonka, MN

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.
