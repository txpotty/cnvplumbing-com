    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after.     Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Minnetonka

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Minnetonka

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load.     Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Minnetonka, MN

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Minnetonka

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on.
