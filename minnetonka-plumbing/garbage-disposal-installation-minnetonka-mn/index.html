    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Minnetonka

    At rough-in we set the drain height and the outlet location before the cabinets go in.     Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Minnetonka

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Most swaps take about an hour.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Minnetonka

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Minnetonka
