    Why Choose CNV Plumbing for Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Minnetonka, MN?

    Digging a sewer the old way means a trench from the house to the street, and every foot of it gets backfilled, compacted and resodded afterward. Trenchless skips most of that work. Our pits run about three feet square, and there are rarely more than two, or none if the cleanout is usable. Less to fix later.

    • Video Proof, You see the damage before the repair and the finished pipe afterward.
    • Less Restoration, Small access points mean less concrete cutting and less sod to replace.
    • Quick Turnaround, Most residential lining jobs are done in a single day.

    Citywide Coverage

    Townhomes and condos with shared laterals are part of our Minnetonka coverage too. Shared lines need HOA sign-off. We help you get that approval in writing, and the camera footage makes it easier for neighbors to agree on who pays for which section of pipe.

    Experienced Team

    The same crew that lines pipe also digs, so they know when a dig is the better call. That honesty saves money. Their experience covers lines under slabs and old trees, plus a few that ran beneath swimming pools and additions built long after the sewer went in.

    Safety-First Approach

    Open pits get barricades and covers at the end of each day. Nobody falls in. Anything deeper than five feet gets shored or sloped, which is the federal OSHA trench rule and the one we follow on every burst no matter how shallow the run looks at first.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Pricing in Minnetonka, MN

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. A dig job looks cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Trenchless avoids most of that. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Sectional Point Repair in Minnetonka

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Minnetonka

    Bursting can upsize the line. A 4-inch clay lateral can be replaced with 6-inch HDPE, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in Minnetonka

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses. The equipment fits in a pickup bed. Compact gear matters on tight lots. The resin is mixed on site, so it does not start curing before the liner is in place.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repairs in Minnetonka

    Sewage coming up through a floor drain is an emergency, but the first fix is usually a cable or a jetter to get the line flowing again. Lining comes after that. CNV Plumbing can often schedule the liner within the same week, once the pipe has been cleaned and measured. In the meantime the house drains normally.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Minnetonka

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The monitor shows us the pipe wall in real time, along with the footage counter, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. That tells us the depth. No guessing where to dig.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Minnetonka, MN

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Calls in Minnetonka

    A sudden collapse usually shows up as every drain in the house backing up at once, often into the lowest tub or floor drain. First we restore flow. Then the camera shows whether the line can be lined or needs a burst. A fully crushed section sometimes needs a short dig before anything else can happen.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair Near Me in Minnetonka, MN

    Freeze and thaw movement can shift older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. Hard or rocky ground makes digging slow and expensive, which is where trenchless pays off. Rocky ground can mean bringing in a hydraulic breaker just to open a trench. A liner put in from the cleanout avoids that cost entirely, and a burst only needs two small pits dug by hand or with a mini excavator.
