    What a Toilet Replacement Covers in Minnetonka, MN

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Minnetonka

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Minnetonka

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then we level it. The base gets caulked, with a small gap left at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Minnetonka, MN

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Minnetonka, MN

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. Multiple toilets can be swapped in a single trip. Tenants keep a working bathroom. Entry gets coordinated with each tenant directly, so you do not have to be there.

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Minnetonka

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. Our picks come down to flush valve size and the MaP score. Parts availability counts too. Proprietary flappers and fill valves mean that ten years from now, a simple fix becomes a special order.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Minnetonka, MN

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. We put both numbers side by side. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Minnetonka, MN

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. We confirm the clearances before ordering. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Minnetonka, MN

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.
