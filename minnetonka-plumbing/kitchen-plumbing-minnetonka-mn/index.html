    Sinks, Disposals and Drains in Minnetonka, MN: How We Handle Them

    Burst Supply Lines in Minnetonka, MN

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put gallons per minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. We replace both sides. The second line is just as old as the one that failed.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair and Replacement in Minnetonka

    A dripping single-handle faucet almost always has a worn cartridge, and Moen, Delta and Kohler all sell the part by model. Common cartridges ride on the truck. When the body is corroded or the pull-down hose is leaking under the sink, a new faucet usually costs close to what the parts and labor would.

    Disposal Repair and Replacement in Minnetonka, MN

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is almost always jammed. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before we run it again. Leaks are a different story. Water from the bottom of the housing means the seals are gone and the unit needs replacing.

    InSinkErator Models We Service in Minnetonka

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which matters in an open kitchen, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. We stock the common sizes. We also service older InSinkErator units when the motor is still sound.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Minnetonka, MN

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before we test it with water. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Minnetonka

    Undermount sinks hang from clips and epoxy under the stone, so we check the mounting before anything else. Drop-in sinks get a bead of silicone under the rim, new strainer baskets and a fresh 1-1/2 inch trap that lines up with the wall stub without any strain on the joints. Supply lines are new every time. We fill both bowls and pull the stoppers to watch for drips.

    New Disposal Installation in Minnetonka

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a new outlet or a switched circuit under the sink, and that part is electrical work. We handle the plumbing and can line up the electrician. Because the disposal outlet is lower than the old tailpiece, the trap usually has to come down an inch or two, and the wall stub has to allow it.

    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Minnetonka

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink typically calls for a trap rated at 20 gallons per minute or more. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. How often it gets cleaned depends on volume. Busy fryer kitchens may need it every few weeks.
