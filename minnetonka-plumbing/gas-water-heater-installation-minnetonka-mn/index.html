    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. CNV Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. We connect the gas, set the flue and check the Guardian flammable vapor sensor and the combustion air path so the safety features work as designed. Draft is checked last.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Cost-Effective Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Minnetonka, MN

    Upgrading an old 0.55 UEF gas tank to a newer model can cut gas use noticeably. We compare models on their uniform energy factor and first-hour rating, then estimate yearly operating cost for your household so you can see the payback. Heavy users save most. We also explain basic upkeep.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka

    Restaurants and laundromats can drain a residential tank in minutes. For businesses we install high-input heaters with bigger gas lines and dedicated vents, sometimes in banks of two or three. Health code sets temperature. Installs can happen after close.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Minnetonka, MN

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We figure your household peak demand from bathrooms and people, then make sure the vent diameter, rise and total length match the manufacturer table and local code. A small vent can backdraft. We test it every time.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. We strap it if code calls for it.
