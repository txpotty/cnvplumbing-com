    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Minnetonka

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Minnetonka, MN

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. The vent changes as well. We plan both on the quote.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka

    Propane homes in Minnetonka are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank. Tank size matters too.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Minnetonka, MN

    Businesses in Minnetonka, MN with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. If one unit goes down, the others keep running. We size the rack by peak demand. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Minnetonka, MN homes every week. Rinnai has a wide model range and good parts support, Navien builds in recirculation on many models, Noritz makes compact condensing units that fit tight spaces, and Rheem pairs well with their tank lineup. Each warranty differs. We explain the differences.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Minnetonka

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka, MN

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Minnetonka, MN homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. Panel capacity gets checked up front.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Minnetonka

    Commercial hot water loads in Minnetonka are heavier and more constant than residential. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. We rack units with a common manifold. One can be serviced while the rest run.
