    What Our Eagan, MN Drain Techs Clean

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Eagan

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Eagan

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot. A blocked main affects every drain in the house, and the lowest fixture shows it first, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning in Eagan, MN

    Restaurants, salons and offices load drains harder than homes do. Grease, hair and paper all show up in volume. We bring heavier sectional machines and jetters that handle 4 and 6 inch lines, and we can schedule work before opening or after close so you do not lose a lunch rush to a plumber in the kitchen.

    Emergency Drain Cleaning in Eagan, MN

    Sewage coming up through a shower drain does not wait for Monday. Our phones are answered after hours too. The tech who shows up has a main line machine and a wet vac on the truck, so the first job is stopping the backup, and the second is getting enough flow back that you can use a toilet again before morning.

    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in Eagan, MN

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. Hair builds a mat under the strainer. For tubs, the cable goes in through the overflow plate rather than the drain, which protects the tub surface, and we clear the trap and the branch beyond it, then run the shower for a few minutes to confirm it drains at full speed with no gurgle.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Eagan, MN

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Drain Repair or Drain Cleaning in Eagan

    Not every clog is just a clog. A cracked or offset pipe clogs again and again. A camera run after cleaning shows whether the line is sound or whether a section has a crack, a belly or a separated joint, and if repair is needed you get an honest quote and the footage to back it up.

    Clogged Toilet Repair in Eagan, MN

If the toilet clears but water still rises in the tub, the clog is past the toilet in the branch or main, and we move to a bigger machine at that point. Kids' toys come out too.
