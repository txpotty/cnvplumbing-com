    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Eagan

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter sized for 4-inch sewer pipe.     Drain Field Line Jetting in Eagan

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut.     Drain Field Repair in Eagan, MN

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Eagan

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. Bad lids get replaced, and risers bring the opening up to grade. Future pumping gets cheaper. Each lid we install gets screws or a lock so a child cannot lift it.
