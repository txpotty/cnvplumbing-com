    Grease Line Jetting in Eagan

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom.     Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Eagan

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Space is tight back there.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Eagan, MN

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. A jetter takes it off the pipe wall. Our jetters run around 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle, which scours the whole inside of the pipe and pushes the loosened grease downstream. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.
