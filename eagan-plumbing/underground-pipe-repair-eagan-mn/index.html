    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Eagan

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Sewer replacements cost more. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on where the pipe runs, how bad the damage is and the method used to fix it. The price comes after we look.

    Many utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment. It is worth asking. Most want proof that the leak was repaired promptly, and we give you an invoice and repair documentation that shows the date, location and fix. Policies vary by utility.

    Water lines get PEX or Type K copper. Each is approved for direct bury. Sewer lines get Schedule 40 PVC, and the final choice depends on the existing pipe, the soil and what your local code allows. We match transitions carefully.

    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a fair DIY job. Service lines are different. Main water lines and sewer laterals require locating, utility marks and code-compliant fittings rated for burial, so those should go to a licensed plumber. Utilities have to be marked before digging.

    It gets worse and more expensive. Soil washes out. A leaking service can undermine a driveway, soak a foundation, grow mold in a crawlspace and run up the water bill every month, while a broken sewer line leaks waste into the yard. Fix it sooner.

    It depends on your policy and the cause. Check what is excluded. Sudden and accidental damage is sometimes covered, but normal aging and long-term leaks generally fall outside coverage. Call your agent to confirm. We provide documentation for the claim.

    For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Both are rated for burial. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, and every one of these holds up to soil and water far better than galvanized or clay. We follow your city's code.

    A spot repair is usually a few hours. Trenchless jobs take about a day. A full sewer replacement with excavation can take two to five days, with footage, depth and inspections setting the pace. We put the schedule in writing.
