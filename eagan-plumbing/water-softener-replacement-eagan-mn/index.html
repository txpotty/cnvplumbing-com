    Softener Systems We Replace in Eagan, MN

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Eagan, MN

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. Outdoor hose bibs usually stay hard. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Eagan

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Eagan

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Softener Loop Installation in Eagan, MN

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Eagan, MN

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Eagan, MN

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Eagan, MN

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Eagan, MN

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Minnesota homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.
