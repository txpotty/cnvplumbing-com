    What Goes Into a Water Heater Replacement in Eagan, MN

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Eagan

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Eagan, MN

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Eagan

    Mid-century houses usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction typically uses PEX and plastic drains. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Eagan, MN

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. Repair stays an option.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Eagan

    Electric water heaters in Eagan usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Eagan, MN

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We install both, and the right pick often comes down to what fits the space, which models carry the warranty length you want and which ones the local supply house keeps on the shelf for fast replacement. We lay out the options.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Eagan

    Bradford White sells through professional plumbers only, so you will not find one at a big-box store. Their tanks have a good reputation for durability, and parts and warranty support run through the plumber who installed it, which makes it simple for us to handle a warranty claim for a homeowner down the road. Supply houses stock them.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Eagan, MN

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Do not wait it out.
