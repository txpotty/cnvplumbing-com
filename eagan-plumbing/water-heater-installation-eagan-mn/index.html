    Gas Water Heater Installation in Eagan, MN

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Eagan, MN

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Eagan, MN

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most.     Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Eagan

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Eagan

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Eagan

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it.     Commercial Water Heater Installation in Eagan

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Installs can happen after close.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Eagan

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Water heater replacements, new fixtures and water or sewer line work typically require a permit and inspection. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.
