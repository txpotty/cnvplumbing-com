    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in St. Louis Park

    Most basic drain clearing in St. Louis Park, MN runs $150 to $500. Main lines cost more than sinks. Jetting costs more than cabling, and sewer repairs run from a few thousand for a spot fix to much more for replacement.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. Clean them when they slow down. Root-prone sewers and grease-heavy kitchens benefit from cleaning every one to two years.

    Some do. Kitchen lines in busy households benefit from a yearly jetting. Root-prone sewers need cutting every 12 to 18 months, and scaled cast iron every couple of years.

    Roots usually enter through an existing crack or joint, then widen it as they grow. Clay pipe can split over time. A root mass can also push a joint out of line, creating an offset that catches waste, and in bad cases the pipe collapses and has to be dug up or lined from the inside.

    Mineral scale inside pipes narrows them and traps grease, hair and soap. Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that discolor fixtures and laundry. Older metal drains have rough walls that collect buildup, layer after layer. A jetter clears the buildup.

    Yes, drain clearing comes with a service guarantee for a set number of days. Repairs and installs get a workmanship warranty. A cracked or root-filled line will clog again, so we explain that before we warranty the clearing.

    Slow sinks and gurgling drains are the early signs. Sewer odor near a drain or cleanout often follows. Outside, look for water that stands more than a day after rain, soggy spots along the foundation, or mulch and soil washed onto the walk, all of which mean water is not leaving the yard the way it should.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It works fast and costs less. Jetting scours the full pipe wall, lasting longer, but it costs more and needs sound pipe.
