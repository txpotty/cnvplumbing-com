    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Businesses in St. Louis Park put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. We schedule around your hours.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. You shower tonight.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is a smart move during other plumbing work. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in St. Louis Park

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park, MN

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in St. Louis Park, MN homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park, MN

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. Repair stays an option.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Electric water heaters in St. Louis Park usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in St. Louis Park, MN

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.
