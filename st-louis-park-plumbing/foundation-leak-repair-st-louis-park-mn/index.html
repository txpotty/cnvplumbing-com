    Locating and Fixing Foundation Leaks in St. Louis Park, MN

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in St. Louis Park, MN

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water leaving a pipe under four inches of concrete. That's the main tool. CNV Plumbing pairs it with a line tracer that follows the pipe route and a thermal camera that shows hot water spreading, so in most homes we can mark the leak within a foot or two, in St. Louis Park, MN or anywhere else.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in St. Louis Park

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. CNV Plumbing makes new crimp, expansion or solvent-weld joints so the line under your St. Louis Park foundation is sound again.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in St. Louis Park, MN

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Look for wet insulation. Standing water under a house rots sills and joists and grows mold on the subfloor, so we repair the source and look at the vapor barrier while we are down there. A sump pump may make sense in a St. Louis Park, MN crawl space that floods from outside.

    Pricing Foundation Leak Repairs in St. Louis Park, MN

    Price depends on where the leak sits and what is on top of it. Tile costs more to open. Once the leak is found, you get one written price for a spot repair and one for a reroute, patching included, so the comparison is fair. That holds for every St. Louis Park, MN job.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in St. Louis Park, MN

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut. From a pit next to the foundation, the crew tunnels by hand to the pipe, a good choice when the leak is under cabinets or expensive tile. CNV Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the St. Louis Park, MN repair is done.

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in St. Louis Park

    Not every wet foundation is a plumbing leak. Gutters cause plenty. Downspouts that dump right at the wall and soil that slopes toward the house push water against the foundation, and that pressure finds every crack in the concrete. We regrade the soil, lengthen the downspouts and install French drains around St. Louis Park homes when needed.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in St. Louis Park, MN

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can finish the surface afterward.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in St. Louis Park
