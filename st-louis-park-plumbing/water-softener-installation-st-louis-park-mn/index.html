    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in St. Louis Park

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff.     Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in St. Louis Park

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in St. Louis Park, MN

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first.     High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in St. Louis Park

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Softener Loop Installation in St. Louis Park

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in St. Louis Park

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.
