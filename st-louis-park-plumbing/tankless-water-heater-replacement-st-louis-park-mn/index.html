    Picking a Tankless for Your St. Louis Park, MN House

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. Starved units throw error codes.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in St. Louis Park, MN

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Insulating vulnerable pipes, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on in unoccupied homes avoid most freeze damage. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Emergency Tankless Replacement in St. Louis Park, MN

    A tankless that will not ignite or leaks from the heat exchanger leaves you with no hot water at all. Call (612) 712-9569 any time it quits. Common models are on the truck. If your exact unit is back-ordered, a temporary repair can sometimes hold until it ships.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in St. Louis Park

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Most take under two hours.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park, MN

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.
