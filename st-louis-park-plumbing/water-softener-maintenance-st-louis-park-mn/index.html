    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in St. Louis Park, MN

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it.     Breaking Up Salt Bridges in St. Louis Park

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge.
