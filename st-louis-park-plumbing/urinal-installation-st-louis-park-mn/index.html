    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in St. Louis Park, MN

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in St. Louis Park

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in St. Louis Park, MN

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. The carrier, 2 inch waste, 3/4 inch supply and vent all go in to the spec sheet before drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in St. Louis Park, MN

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. We run the drain to the nearest stack or building drain, add a vent or an air admittance valve where local code allows one, and pick a compact bowl that fits the space without a full commercial carrier. Unheated garages need freeze planning. Ask us about the layout.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in St. Louis Park

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in St. Louis Park

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We lay out each urinal at 30 inches center to center and 15 inches from the side wall, set the carriers, and run waste, vent and supply so the finish crew can hang fixtures as soon as tile is done. Coordination with the GC is constant. Rough inspection comes first.

Optima sensor valves fit the same spud. Battery models need no wiring.
