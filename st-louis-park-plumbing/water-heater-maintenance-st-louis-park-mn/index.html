    What a Water Heater Tune-Up Covers in St. Louis Park, MN

    Tank Flush and Inspection in St. Louis Park

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. The tank heats better. So does recovery time.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in St. Louis Park

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that stain fixtures and laundry. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in St. Louis Park, MN

    Something sounds or feels wrong. If the tank is rumbling, the hot water ran out early or there is water in the pan, we can come out, find the cause and tell you whether it is a simple repair or a sign the tank is near the end. You get options. The call is yours.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in St. Louis Park, MN

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in St. Louis Park, MN

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Maintenance helps a tank reach its full rated life and helps tankless units last much longer. Old tanks may be done. We give you real numbers for both.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in St. Louis Park

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Descaling Tankless Units in St. Louis Park

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. We descale through the service valves. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Anode Rod Replacement in St. Louis Park

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. Spent rods get replaced. It keeps rust off the tank lining.
