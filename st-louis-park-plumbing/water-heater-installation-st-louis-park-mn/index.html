    Electric Water Heater Installation in St. Louis Park, MN

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in St. Louis Park, MN

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in St. Louis Park

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Minnesota has a statewide plumbing code based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, and plumbers must be licensed by the state. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in St. Louis Park, MN

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Expansion Tank Installation in St. Louis Park, MN

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
