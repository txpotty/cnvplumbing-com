    Annual Tankless Checkup in St. Louis Park, MN

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in St. Louis Park

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in St. Louis Park

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in St. Louis Park, MN

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in St. Louis Park

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Noritz Descaling in St. Louis Park, MN

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in St. Louis Park

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.
