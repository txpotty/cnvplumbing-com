    Sump Pump Maintenance in St. Louis Park, MN Ahead of Storm Season

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in St. Louis Park, MN

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. We clean their screens, test the floats and follow the discharge until it reaches daylight or an approved drain, because a line that dead-ends under the house just moves the water around. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages still freeze during cold snaps.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in St. Louis Park

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in St. Louis Park

    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise groundwater fast in Minnesota, sending water into basements and overloading sump pumps. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in St. Louis Park, MN

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. Rattling or a high whine at startup usually means the motor is near the end.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in St. Louis Park, MN

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in St. Louis Park

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. We match the float and seals to the model number on the label. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in St. Louis Park, MN

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in St. Louis Park

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.
