    Every Kind of Copper Leak, Repaired in St. Louis Park, MN

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in St. Louis Park, MN

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages still freeze during cold snaps. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Insulation goes on after.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in St. Louis Park

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, so we locate them with a thermal camera and moisture meter before cutting a single opening in the wall or ceiling. We replace the pitted length with Type L. We look at the copper around it.

    Corrosion Leak Repair in St. Louis Park, MN

    Water can be hard on copper over time. Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that stain fixtures and laundry. Thinning walls lead to leaks. We look at corrosion patterns on the pipe we remove, since the pattern often points to the cause. Then we fix that too.

    Copper Leak Behind Walls in St. Louis Park, MN

    Small pinholes can suddenly widen and spray water into a wall or ceiling. Locate your shutoff. We help you shut off the water over the phone and send a plumber to locate and repair the leak, and if the damage is extensive, we make a safe temporary fix to restore water while we plan the full repair. You will know what needs drying.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in St. Louis Park, MN

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. It takes about an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, and run a thermal scan on walls where pipe runs if the house has had leaks before. We write up what we find.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in St. Louis Park, MN

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. Less run time, less wear.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in St. Louis Park

    Copper often runs under concrete slabs in St. Louis Park homes. Ground movement pulls on it. Freeze and thaw movement can crack older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. We use acoustic and thermal equipment to find the leak without breaking concrete to search, so you can choose between a targeted slab cut and abandoning the line for a new route. Each gets a quote.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in St. Louis Park, MN

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Freeze and thaw movement can crack older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. Look for white crust. We inspect each joint near the leak for weeping or mineral buildup, then clean, reflux and resolder the joint, or replace the fitting entirely with a new sweat or press fitting. Every joint gets tested.
