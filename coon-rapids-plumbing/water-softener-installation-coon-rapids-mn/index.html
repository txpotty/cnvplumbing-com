    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Coon Rapids, MN

    Building a home in Coon Rapids, MN? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Coon Rapids

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Coon Rapids

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron changes the math. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Coon Rapids

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Coon Rapids

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Coon Rapids, MN

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Coon Rapids

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Coon Rapids

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.
