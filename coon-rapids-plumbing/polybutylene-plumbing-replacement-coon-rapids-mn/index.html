    Why Polybutylene Fails in Coon Rapids

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs showerheads, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Coon Rapids

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.
