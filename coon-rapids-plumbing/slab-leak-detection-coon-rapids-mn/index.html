    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Coon Rapids, MN

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Coon Rapids, MN

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.
