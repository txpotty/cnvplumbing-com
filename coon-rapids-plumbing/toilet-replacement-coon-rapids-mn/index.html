    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Coon Rapids, MN

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Coon Rapids, MN

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.
