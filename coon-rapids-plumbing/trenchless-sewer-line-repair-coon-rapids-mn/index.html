    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Coon Rapids

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. So the line gets jetted or descaled to bare wall before any resin goes in.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Coon Rapids

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Coon Rapids, MN

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Coon Rapids

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.
