    Pressure Switch Replacement in Coon Rapids

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Coon Rapids, MN

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Coon Rapids, MN

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. CNV Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Coon Rapids

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. The motor, the wire splice and the check valve are the other common failures. Before pulling anything, we test the motor and wire from the wellhead. New pumps match your well's yield.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Coon Rapids

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.
