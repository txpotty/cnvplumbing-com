    Jetting a Sewer Line in Plymouth, MN

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Plymouth, MN

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. A line that has backed up before can be cleaned on a set schedule, often every year or two, so it never stops again without warning.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Plymouth, MN

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Plymouth

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Plymouth, MN

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Plymouth, MN
