    Everyday Faucet Repairs in Plymouth, MN

    Ball Faucet Repair in Plymouth, MN

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Plymouth, MN

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Plymouth

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Plymouth

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Plymouth, MN

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Plymouth

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Plymouth, MN

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Look at the floor of the sink cabinet. We replace the O-rings and tighten the mounting hardware, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Plymouth, MN

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.
