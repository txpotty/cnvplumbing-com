    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Plymouth

    Standard drain snaking in Plymouth, MN usually costs $150 to $500 depending on where the clog sits and how easy it is to reach.     Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting works another way. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls clean. Many tough lines do best with both, cut first to open the line and jetted second to wash out what the blades left.

    Chemicals sometimes open a small hole in a hair clog, but they will not touch roots or a grease plug. Repeated use can weaken old PVC joints and pit cast iron. They are dangerous to handle. A cable takes the clog out instead of melting a narrow path through it, which keeps the drain open longer.

    Plan on 1 to 3 hours for a single clogged drain. A root-choked main or a long jetting job may take 3 to 6 hours. We tell you up front. After looking at the cleanout and hearing which drains are slow, the tech can narrow the estimate.

    No permit is needed for routine rooting. Installing a new cleanout, digging up a broken section or replacing the sewer lateral usually does require a plumbing permit and an inspection. Permits for repair work are on us.

    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots chase that moisture to the joint and work their way inside. Inside, they thicken fast. In Plymouth, clay pipe with a joint every 2 to 3 feet and older cast iron with oakum joints are the most common targets.
