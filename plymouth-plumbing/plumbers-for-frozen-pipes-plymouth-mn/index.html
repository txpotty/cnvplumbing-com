    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Plymouth, MN

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Plymouth, MN

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a hose bib with the hose left on. A connected hose holds water in the stem. Irrigation backflow preventers sit above ground with no insulation, and their brass bodies and test cocks crack easily, so we drain or cover them before the first freeze and repair them when they split.

    Preventive Pipe Winterization in Plymouth

    Winterizing before the first hard freeze costs far less than a burst pipe repair. We walk the house and mark every exposed run. Attic lines get foam insulation, crawlspace vents get closed or covered, outdoor faucets get swapped for frost-free models, and irrigation lines get drained or blown out before the cold arrives.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Plymouth

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Plymouth

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. You will see the backup first. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.
