    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Plymouth

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Plymouth, MN

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn both oval handles under the sink clockwise until they stop, and if they spin freely or keep weeping, go to the main shutoff and close it before calling us.     Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Plymouth, MN

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Plymouth

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Plymouth, MN

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Plymouth, MN

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Plymouth

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount bowls hang from clips epoxied to the stone, and pedestal sinks rely on a wall bracket that may have pulled out of old drywall over the years. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Plymouth, MN

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.
