    Why Polybutylene Fails in Plymouth

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs showerheads, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
