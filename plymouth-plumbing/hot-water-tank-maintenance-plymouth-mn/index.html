    Standard Tank Flushing in Plymouth

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters.     Standard Tank Tune-Up in Plymouth

    Electric Thermostat Service in Plymouth

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. A tripped reset is a symptom. We trace the cause, often a shorted element or a stuck thermostat, before resetting it. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Plymouth

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. A capped discharge pipe is a serious hazard.
