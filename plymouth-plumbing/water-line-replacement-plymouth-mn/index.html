    Copper Water Line Installation in Plymouth

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. It costs more than PEX.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Plymouth

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Plymouth, MN

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    Emergency Water Line Repair and Replacement in Plymouth, MN

    If water is flooding the yard or the taps are dry, call (612) 712-9569. We find the break, clamp or splice it if the pipe can hold, and give you a straight answer on whether the line needs a full replacement, along with a date to do it. Overnight fixes happen.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Plymouth

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Plymouth
