    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Plymouth

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Plymouth, MN

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Plymouth, MN homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Plymouth

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When replacing one, we match fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty still applies, and register the new heater right away. Registration matters.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Plymouth, MN

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We lay out the options.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Plymouth, MN

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. Repair stays an option.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Plymouth

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Plymouth, MN

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Plymouth

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. Hot water by evening.
