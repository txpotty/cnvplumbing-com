    Jetting Equipment We Use in Plymouth, MN

    Different lines need different machines. Small-line jetters for branch drains and larger units up to 4,000 PSI for sewers cover most jobs. Nozzle choice matters most. Penetrating heads bore through packed clogs, rear-jet heads scrub walls, and spinning cutters handle roots.
