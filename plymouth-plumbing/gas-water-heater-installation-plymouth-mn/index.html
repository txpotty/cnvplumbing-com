    Gas Water Heater Installation Cost in Plymouth, MN

    An atmospheric vent tank is the cheapest gas install. Power vent and tankless units cost more, and vent changes, gas line upgrades and code corrections on older installs can add to any job. Permits and haul-away are included. We also compare gas and electric operating costs.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Plymouth, MN

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Plymouth, MN

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Plymouth

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We call the propane company when needed. Every joint is leak tested.
