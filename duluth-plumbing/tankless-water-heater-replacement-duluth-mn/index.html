    Picking a Tankless for Your Duluth, MN Home

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Duluth, MN

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Expect a full day.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Duluth

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is the fix, and we can often have a new unit running the same or next day.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Duluth

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Duluth

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Summers can be warm and humid, bringing condensation on cold water lines in basements and utility rooms. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Duluth

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Duluth

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Duluth

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Duluth

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.
