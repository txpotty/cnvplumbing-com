    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Duluth

    Electric water heaters in Duluth usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Duluth, MN

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. Repair stays an option.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Duluth

    Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is worth considering during other plumbing work. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Duluth

    Businesses in Duluth run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. We schedule around your hours.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Duluth, MN

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Do not wait it out.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Duluth

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. Registration matters.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Duluth, MN

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Duluth

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.
