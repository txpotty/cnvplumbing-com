    Proper Sizing and Venting in Duluth, MN

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. Undersized vents spill CO. We test it every time.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Duluth

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. Strapping where required. Your household sets the size.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Installation in Duluth, MN

    No hot water and the pilot will not stay lit? CNV Plumbing can usually get there quickly across Duluth, MN. We check whether a repair like a thermocouple or a gas valve will do before recommending a new unit. A dead tank gets replaced.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Duluth

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.
