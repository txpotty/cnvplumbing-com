    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Duluth, MN

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Duluth, MN

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Duluth, MN

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Duluth, MN

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. Each new appliance gets a properly sized branch, a shutoff you can reach, a drip leg where required and a new coated connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Duluth

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and perimeter drains are central to keeping homes dry. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Duluth

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Duluth, MN

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. It takes about an hour. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave.
