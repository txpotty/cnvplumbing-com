    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Duluth

    Cost depends on whether it is a clog or a construction job. Clearing a fixture or main line is a few hundred dollars at most. Camera inspections, jetting and sewer repairs step up from there, and yard systems like French drains, channel drains or dry wells are priced by the length of drain and the amount of digging involved. We quote in writing first.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It is quick and cheap. Jetting scours the full pipe wall, lasting longer, but it costs more and needs sound pipe.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. Jetters are trailer or cart units. Good equipment matters because a camera with a locator tells us exactly where and how deep a problem is, which keeps a sewer repair to one small excavation instead of a long trench.

    A single fixture drain usually takes about an hour. Plan on one to two hours for a main line. Camera work adds 30 to 60 minutes, and a buried or missing cleanout adds more.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. They only need a hairline gap. Once a root tip gets in through a cracked joint or loose seal, it grows into a mass that catches paper and grease, and clay and older concrete pipe with many joints are the most vulnerable.

    Watch for water pooling near the house after storms. Washed-out mulch tells you too. A damp crawlspace or white crust on the foundation means water is reaching the house.

    Simple downspout extensions are a reasonable DIY project. Trench drains take more skill. A French drain or dry well needs correct slope, the right fabric and stone, and an outlet that will not dump water on a neighbor, and digging without an 811 locate risks hitting a gas or power line.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. A main line needs more time. A main line with camera work usually takes about two hours, more if the cleanout is hard to reach.
