    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Duluth, MN

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Duluth, MN

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Duluth

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Hold off on running water. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Duluth

    Backups at night or on weekends are handled with the same gear as any weekday call. On-call trucks are fully equipped. That means we can clear and jet a grease-packed restaurant line or a root-filled home sewer in one visit, without waiting until Monday to bring the jetter back for a second trip.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Duluth

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Duluth, MN

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Duluth

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Duluth, MN

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise groundwater fast in Minnesota, sending water into basements and overloading sump pumps. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.
