    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Duluth, MN

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Duluth, MN

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. We cut it out with an auger. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Duluth

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Duluth, MN

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Duluth

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Duluth, MN

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. Both ride on our trucks. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Duluth, MN
