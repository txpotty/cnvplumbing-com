    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Duluth, MN

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Duluth, MN

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Water heaters and boilers work hard through the heating season, which adds wear compared to milder climates.     Foundation Crack Repair in Duluth

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.
