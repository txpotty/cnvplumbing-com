    Full Home PEX Repiping in Duluth

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Duluth

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Duluth, MN

    Gray polybutylene was installed widely from the late seventies into the mid nineties. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Duluth

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
