    Disposal Repair in Duluth, MN: Symptom by Symptom

    Humming Disposal Repair in Duluth

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Duluth, MN

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. Common kits ride on our trucks. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Duluth

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Duluth, MN

    A disposal repair that costs half of a new unit rarely makes sense, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Duluth

    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Duluth, MN

    A drip at the top usually means the sink flange putty has dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. That fixes most top leaks. A leak on the side is usually the discharge gasket or a loose hose clamp.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Duluth, MN

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Duluth

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.
