    Waterproofing for Older Minnesota Homes in Duluth, MN

    Older basements were often built with block walls and no exterior membrane. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is a smart move during other plumbing work.     Groundwater Pressure Relief in Duluth

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. You may see water coming up at the floor cracks or around the floor drain during a long wet week. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Duluth, MN

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Duluth

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Duluth

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.
