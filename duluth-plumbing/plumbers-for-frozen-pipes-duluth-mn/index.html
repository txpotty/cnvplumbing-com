    Post-Thaw Pipe Inspection & Repair in Duluth, MN

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Duluth, MN

    Finding the frozen spot is the hard part. Thermal cameras make it faster. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.

    Pool, Outdoor Equipment and Water Heater Line Freeze Damage in Duluth, MN

    Pool equipment lines, outdoor kitchen sinks and ice maker tubing in garages are easy to forget until they crack. Pool pumps often run to prevent freezing.     Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Duluth

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Brass bodies crack. We repair or replace the damaged assembly, have it tested if your water utility requires it, and show you how to drain it or fit an insulated cover before the next freeze.
