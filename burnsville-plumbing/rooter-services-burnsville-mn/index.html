    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots chase that moisture to the joint and work their way inside. Inside, they thicken fast. In Burnsville, clay with joints every couple of feet and old cast iron with lead and oakum joints get hit the most.

    Most rooter jobs include a limited guarantee for a set number of days. A repeat clog in that window means a free return visit.
