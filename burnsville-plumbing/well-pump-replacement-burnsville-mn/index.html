    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. The switch itself is a cheap part. We show you the burned contacts.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Burnsville, MN

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime.     Pulling a Submersible Pump in Burnsville
