    Tank and Tankless Water Heaters in Burnsville, MN

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Burnsville, MN

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Leak checks follow. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Burnsville

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Burnsville, MN

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Burnsville

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. We schedule around your hours. Night installs are available.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Burnsville, MN

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Burnsville

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. We follow them exactly. That protects your coverage.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Burnsville, MN

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Burnsville

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.
