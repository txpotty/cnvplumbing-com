    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Burnsville

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and the cable only bores a narrow path through the middle that closes up again within weeks. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Burnsville

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Many lines stay clear for years after.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Burnsville

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes snag on the coating and roots growing through a joint hold the whole mass in place until the line stops. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Burnsville, MN

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they make a plug that a cable can only partly clear. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.

    Identifying and Repairing Damaged Pipes in Burnsville

    A clog that keeps coming back usually means broken pipe. No amount of clearing fixes a crack. Our camera finds the crack, offset or collapse, while the locator marks it so the repair can be quoted right. CNV Plumbing gives you options from a single dig to full replacement.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Burnsville

    Some older homes have no cleanout at all. That makes every clog harder. Without one, a plumber has to pull a toilet or climb on the roof to reach the main, so we install a two-way cleanout near the foundation that gives direct access upstream and downstream. Future clearing gets cheaper and faster.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Burnsville, MN

    Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and foundation drainage are central to keeping homes dry. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Waste settles in the low spot. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, so the clog keeps returning in the same place. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.
