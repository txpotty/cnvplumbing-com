    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Burnsville, MN

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some.     Running Toilet Fixes in Burnsville

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Burnsville, MN

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way. Some early 1.6 gallon toilets from the 1990s just flush poorly, and we will say so.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Burnsville, MN

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Burnsville, MN
