    Why Polybutylene Fails in Burnsville

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs showerheads, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Burnsville, MN

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Budget and timing matter. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. You will know exactly what remains.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Burnsville

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. A repair buys time.     Full Home Repiping with PEX in Burnsville

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings.
