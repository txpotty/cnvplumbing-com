    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Burnsville, MN

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Burnsville, MN

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Burnsville, MN

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Burnsville, MN

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is worth considering during other plumbing work. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Root Masses in Burnsville Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Burnsville

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.
