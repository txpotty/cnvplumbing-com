    Commercial Water Line Services in Burnsville, MN

    Besides repairs, CNV Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, like a restaurant adding a dish line or a second restroom. Bigger meters need bigger pipe.     PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Burnsville

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. With PEX, the fitting is the weak point most of the time. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Burnsville, MN

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Underground Leak Detection in Burnsville, MN

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Some leaks barely make a sound. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Freeze and thaw movement can shift older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. You get a written price as soon as the leak is marked on the ground.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Burnsville

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Burnsville, MN

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. CNV Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. Most breaks are shut down quickly. If the repair will run long, a temporary bypass keeps water flowing to the house during the repair.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Burnsville, MN

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around Burnsville should test for hardness and iron before sizing a treatment system. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Burnsville

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.
