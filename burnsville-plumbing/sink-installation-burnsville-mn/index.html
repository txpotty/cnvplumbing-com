    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Burnsville, MN

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Burnsville, MN

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Burnsville, MN

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Burnsville, MN

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Utility Sink Installation in Burnsville

    Laundry and garage tubs take dirty water and a lot of it. We set the tub on a sturdy stand or wall bracket, run a 1-1/2 inch trap so sand and lint do not stop it up, and add a faucet that a bucket fits under. Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that stain fixtures and laundry. A cleanout plug goes on the trap.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Burnsville

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Burnsville, MN

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Burnsville

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Burnsville, MN

    Labor is the main cost driver. Moving the drain or adding a second bowl adds hours, and a straight drop-in swap is the cheapest. The sink price is separate. You can buy it or we can.
