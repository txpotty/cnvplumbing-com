    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Burnsville, MN

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Burnsville, MN

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. A locator marks the depth. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and perimeter drains are central to keeping homes dry.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Burnsville

    Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs showerheads, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Burnsville

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. We send the report to your agent too. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Burnsville, MN

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Burnsville, MN

    Older homes in Burnsville often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. We mark it on the ground. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Burnsville, MN

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Burnsville, MN

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Inspectors like documentation. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Burnsville

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.
