    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Burnsville

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Burnsville, MN

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished.     Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Burnsville

A jetter actually takes it out of the line.
