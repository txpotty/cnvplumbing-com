    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Burnsville, MN

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Burnsville, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. We follow the manufacturer's steps, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Burnsville

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Burnsville

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A heavily scaled unit burns more gas per shower than a clean one, and that adds up each month. We lay out the maintenance price next to the likely repair cost so you can decide.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Burnsville, MN

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. Descaling costs far less. Cared for, many units run 15 to 20 years. We will also say whether a softener or a scale inhibitor makes sense for your water.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Burnsville, MN

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Burnsville

    Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that stain fixtures and laundry. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Burnsville

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.
