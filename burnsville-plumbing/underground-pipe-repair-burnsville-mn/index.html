    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Burnsville

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, and buried runs tend to split at fittings or where the pipe was bent tight during installation. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Underground Water Line Repair in Burnsville, MN

    A leaking water service runs at full street pressure around the clock. The meter never stops. Low pressure, a wet spot that never dries and a meter that turns with everything off are the usual clues, so we verify it with a pressure test and then pinpoint it acoustically. Repairs use approved fittings.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Burnsville

    Buried sewer damage shows up as odor, slow drains or a soggy patch over the line. Video shows the break. We run a camera with a sonde through the lateral, mark the break on the surface and repair it with a spot dig, a sectional liner or full replacement depending on what the footage shows. Trenchless methods often save Burnsville driveways. We tell you if yours qualifies.

    Emergency Underground Pipe Repair in Burnsville

    A burst service line can flood a yard and wash out the soil under a driveway. Turn off water at the meter.     Main Water Line Repair in Burnsville

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Small leaks grow. A spinning meter dial with every fixture off, falling pressure or a wet spot that never dries all point to a main line leak, and we locate it, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. Rusted steel is better replaced.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Burnsville, MN
