    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Replacing a section of sewer, adding a cleanout, or connecting a new drain to the sewer generally requires a permit and an inspection, and we handle that paperwork and schedule the inspector as part of the job.

    Snaking is the right first step for most single clogs. It is fast and inexpensive. Jetting wins for grease, scale and recurring roots since it cleans the whole wall.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It works fast and costs less.
