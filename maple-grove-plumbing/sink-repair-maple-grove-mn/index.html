    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Maple Grove, MN

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Maple Grove, MN

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Maple Grove, MN

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Maple Grove, MN

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Maple Grove

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Maple Grove, MN

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. Close the angle stops while you wait. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Maple Grove

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Look under the sink twice a year. Feel for moisture at the trap and lines, and turn the stops now and then so they do not seize. Two minutes is plenty.
