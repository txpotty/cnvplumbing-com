    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Maple Grove

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon gas tank usually runs $1,800 to $3,200 installed, haul-away and basic code upgrades included. Power vent and 75 gallon tanks run $3,000 to $4,800. The price is firm before we start.

    Tank warranties usually run 6 to 10 years, with 1 to 2 years on parts like the gas valve and igniter. Our labor warranty covers the install. Keep the receipt.

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising up a metal flue on its own. Power vent models use a fan to push exhaust out through plastic pipe, which lets them vent sideways through a wall and often burn a bit more efficiently. A power vent needs electricity.

    Most swaps run three to five hours. Converting to a power vent or moving the heater to a new spot can take most of a day, since it involves new vent and gas piping. The timeline is part of the quote.
