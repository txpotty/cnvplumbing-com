    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Maple Grove

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Maple Grove, MN

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Maple Grove

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Maple Grove, MN

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Maple Grove, MN

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Maple Grove

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Hold off on running water. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Maple Grove

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Maple Grove, MN

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.
