    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Maple Grove, MN

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Maple Grove, MN

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Maple Grove

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Maple Grove, MN

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Maple Grove, MN

    Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs showerheads, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. By that point the threads that hold the trim are often too weak to grip a new part, and the valve body itself may be pitted where the cartridge seals against it. A new valve makes more sense.

    Tub Valves in Older Houses in Maple Grove, MN

    Cast iron tubs and plaster walls change the plan. Plaster cracks wider than drywall when you cut it, so we open plaster carefully with a multi-tool and often go in from an adjacent closet, and the heavy tub stays put while we work on the valve above it. Patching plaster is slow. Access panels avoid it.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Maple Grove

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Maple Grove

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.
