    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Maple Grove, MN

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Softener Brands We Work On in Maple Grove

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. The valve matters more than the brand. Kinetico and Culligan use their own designs, so we check part availability before quoting.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Maple Grove

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. We trace the inlet and outlet plumbing, the bypass, the drain line and the brine overflow. Green crust on copper fittings, a drip at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Maple Grove, MN

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Maple Grove, MN

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. Code requires an air gap at the drain.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Maple Grove, MN

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Maple Grove, MN

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Maple Grove

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. Cheaper than a new head.
