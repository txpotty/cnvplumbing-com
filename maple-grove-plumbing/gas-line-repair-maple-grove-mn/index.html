    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Maple Grove

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Maple Grove, MN

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. Each new appliance gets a properly sized branch, a shutoff you can reach, a drip leg where required and a new coated connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Maple Grove, MN

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any finished basement.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Maple Grove
