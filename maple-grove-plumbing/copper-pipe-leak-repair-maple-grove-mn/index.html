    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Maple Grove

    A minor, accessible pinhole repair starts around $200. Hidden leaks cost more. Complex repairs like under-slab leaks can run past $2,000, with location, severity and footage of new pipe setting most of the price. You get a written number after locating.

    We typically use Type L copper. It has a thicker wall. Type L lasts longer than the Type M found in many older homes, while press fittings or a PEX transition get used when a torch is a bad idea. Every part is lead-free.

    Keep water pressure under about 80 psi. A pressure valve does it. Treating hard or aggressive water, insulating pipe in cold spots and limiting how long a recirculation pump runs slow down the corrosion that thins copper. Know where your main shutoff is.

    Keep pressure in range. Test it yearly. Soften hard water, limit recirculation pump run time and get any green crust on fittings looked at early, while homes with older copper should have it checked every few years. Small fixes now save drywall later.

    A small isolated repair usually does not need one. Bigger jobs might. Replacing a large section, rerouting lines or working on the main water supply in Maple Grove may require a plumbing permit, which allows an inspection before anything is covered. We pull it when needed.

    An accessible pinhole or a single joint takes one to four hours. Slab leaks take longer. Leaks under a slab or a repipe can take a full day or more, depending on access, whether the line is rerouted and how much copper needs replacing. The quote includes a time estimate.

    Most accessible repairs take one to two hours. Walls add time. Leaks inside walls or ceilings can take two to four hours, while slab repairs can take most of a day. You hear the schedule up front.
