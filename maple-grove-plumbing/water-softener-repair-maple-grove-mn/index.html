    Resin Testing and Replacement in Maple Grove

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Maple Grove

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.
