    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Maple Grove

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Maple Grove

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Maple Grove

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Maple Grove, MN

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. We plan the layout first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Maple Grove, MN

    Budget showers can still be built right. A one-piece unit with a good valve is a fraction of the cost of tile, and set properly on mortar it holds up for a long time. The valve is where to spend. You get the full price before we start.

    Shower Stall Installation in Maple Grove

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.
