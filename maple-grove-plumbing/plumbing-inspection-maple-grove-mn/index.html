    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Maple Grove, MN

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. A locator marks the depth. Freeze and thaw movement can shift older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Maple Grove, MN

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Maple Grove

    Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that stain fixtures and laundry. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Maple Grove

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Minnesota houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. We test each one directly. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Maple Grove

    Businesses in Maple Grove use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. We look at backflow assemblies, water heaters, restroom flush valves, floor drains and trap primers and the building sewer. Downtime costs money. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Maple Grove, MN

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Maple Grove, MN

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Maple Grove, MN

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Our technicians inspect all major water heater brands, including Rheem, AO Smith and Bradford White. Tankless is included. We also check Rinnai and Noritz tankless heaters and the common fixture brands found in most homes, so the brand on your equipment will not limit what we can inspect.

    A plumbing inspection is a deeper look at one system. General inspectors check basic function. Home inspectors run water and flush toilets, and our plumbers add sewer video, pressure tests, pipe identification and code review.

    Every one to two years works for most homes. Some houses need it yearly. Houses with original pipe, big trees over the sewer or past leaks do better with a yearly check that catches problems while they are small.
