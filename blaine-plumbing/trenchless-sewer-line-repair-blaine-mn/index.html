    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Blaine, MN

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Blaine, MN

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and perimeter drains are central to keeping homes dry. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.
