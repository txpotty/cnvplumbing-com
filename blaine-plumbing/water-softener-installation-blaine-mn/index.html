    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Blaine

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Blaine

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Blaine

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water.
