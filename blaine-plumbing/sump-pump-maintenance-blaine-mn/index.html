    Battery Backup System Testing in Blaine, MN

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Blaine

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Blaine, MN

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Blaine

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Blaine

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Blaine

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne need different checks. We know both.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Blaine, MN

A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.
