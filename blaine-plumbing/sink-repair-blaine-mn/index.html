    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Blaine, MN

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Blaine

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Blaine, MN

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Blaine, MN

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Blaine

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Blaine, MN

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.
