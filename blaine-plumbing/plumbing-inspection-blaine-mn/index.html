    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Blaine, MN

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Blaine, MN

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and perimeter drains are central to keeping homes dry.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Blaine, MN

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Blaine

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Blaine, MN

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Blaine

    Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that discolor fixtures and laundry. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Blaine, MN

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Water Heater Inspections in Blaine

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Blaine

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Buyers use our inspection to learn the pipe type, the sewer condition and the heater's age before the option period ends. That leaves room to negotiate. Galvanized supply lines, a cracked clay sewer or a water heater past fifteen years are all things a seller can credit for or fix before closing.
