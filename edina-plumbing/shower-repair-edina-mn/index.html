    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Edina

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Edina, MN

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable.     Weak Shower Spray Repair in Edina, MN

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Edina

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head.     Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Edina, MN

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. Then we fix the source. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Edina

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck.
