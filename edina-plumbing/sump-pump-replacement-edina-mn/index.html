    Backup Pump Options in Edina, MN

    A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any finished basement. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. A backup takes over when the main pump quits or loses power. Water-powered backups use city water pressure and need no battery, but they only make sense with strong municipal pressure and an approved backflow preventer on the line. They will not work on a well.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Edina

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. The rest is simple arithmetic. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Edina

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Edina

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Edina

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Edina

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Edina, MN

An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.
