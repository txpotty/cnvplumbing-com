    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Edina

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Edina

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Edina, MN

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Edina

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Edina, MN

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Edina

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Edina

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Edina, MN

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. Descaling costs far less.
