    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Edina

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Edina, MN

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Edina

    Electric water heaters in Edina usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Edina, MN

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Edina, MN homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Edina, MN

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect.
