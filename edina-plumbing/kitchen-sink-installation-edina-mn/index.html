    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Edina

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Edina, MN

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive.     Apron-Front Sinks in Edina, MN

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated.     Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Edina

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Edina, MN

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Edina

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel.     Drop-In Sink Replacement in Edina
