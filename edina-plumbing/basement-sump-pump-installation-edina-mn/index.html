    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Edina

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. A brand new pit through the slab falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and a battery backup adds roughly $600 to $1,500. Most people add the backup.

    Look for a pump that runs nonstop, short cycles, or does not start when the pit fills. Corrosion on the housing and a float that hangs up are warning signs, and if your basement flooded even though you have a pump, the pump or its switch was not up to the job. Over seven years old is suspect.

    Discharge belongs in the yard, a dry well, or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Tying a sump pump into the sanitary sewer is not allowed in most places, and it can draw a fine.

    Liberty Pumps, Zoeller and Wayne are our regulars. Each offers cast iron housings and dependable switches, and all three have solid parts support through local supply houses, which matters more than a fancy feature list when something breaks. The pit size picks the model.

    Submersibles sit in the pit, sealed, and run quiet. A pedestal mounts the motor on a column above the water, which makes them cheaper and easier to service but louder, so they work best in an unfinished utility room rather than a finished family room. Both move water fine.

    A straight pump replacement generally goes without a permit. New pits, new discharge routes and new circuits may, so we check the local rules and pull permits when they apply.

    We strongly recommend professional installation. You are dealing with wiring, pipe and concrete in one job, and a bad seal on the lid, an undersized pit or a discharge that drains toward the house can leave you worse off than having no pump at all. Errors appear at the worst moment.

    Permit rules vary by town, including Edina. A straight pump replacement usually does not, but a new pit, a new outlet or a new exit through the foundation wall often does, and we sort that out before any concrete gets cut. We handle the paperwork.
