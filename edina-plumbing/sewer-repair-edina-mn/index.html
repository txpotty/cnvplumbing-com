    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Edina

    Most jobs fall between $500 for a small spot repair and about $3,000 for a trenchless section. Replacing the whole line is a bigger number.     Schedule 40 PVC is our standard for open trench work. PVC will not corrode. For pipe bursting we use fused HDPE, which comes in continuous lengths with no joints for roots to find, and for lining we use epoxy liners rated for decades of service inside the old pipe. We follow what your city's code allows.

    A spot repair or a jetting job usually takes a few hours. Pipe lining runs one to two days. A full replacement with excavation can take two to five days, with length, depth and the city inspection schedule setting the pace. The schedule is in the written estimate.

    Our labor warranty is written into the quote. Length depends on the repair. A spot repair with new PVC and a full lining job carry different terms because the materials and risks differ, and we explain exactly what is covered and for how long before the work starts. Manufacturer warranties stack on top.

    Major repairs and replacements in Edina, MN usually require one. Minor work can be exempt. Rules differ on whether a short spot repair on private property needs a permit, while work at the street or the public tap nearly always needs one. We check with the city on every job.

    We use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS that meets local code. Old-to-new joints get stainless shielded bands. The coupling has to match the outside diameter of both pipes, and for lining work we use epoxy-saturated liners that cure into a rigid, jointless pipe inside the host. All materials are rated for buried sewer use.
