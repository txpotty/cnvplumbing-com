    Hydro-Jetting Services in Edina, MN

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, while a jetter removes the grease, scale and fine roots coating the pipe so the next clog has nothing to start on. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Edina

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, and the transmitter lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above it. It saves guessing where to dig. You get a copy of the footage.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Edina

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line.
