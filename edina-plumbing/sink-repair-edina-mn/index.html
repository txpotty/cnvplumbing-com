    Common Sink Repairs in Edina, MN

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Edina, MN

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Edina

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount bowls hang from clips epoxied to the stone, and pedestal sinks rely on a wall bracket that may have pulled out of old drywall over the years. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Edina, MN

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Edina

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Edina

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Edina

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Edina

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Insulating vulnerable pipes, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on in unoccupied homes prevent most freeze damage. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Edina, MN

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
