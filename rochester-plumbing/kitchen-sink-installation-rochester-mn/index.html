    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Rochester, MN: Style by Style

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Rochester

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Rochester

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Rochester get their commercial sinks installed by CNV Plumbing. Hand sinks need hot and cold. Three-compartment, prep and hand sinks go in with the floor sinks, indirect drains and grease trap tie-ins the inspector checks.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Rochester, MN

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Rochester

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Composite Sink Installation in Rochester

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Rochester, MN

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Rochester

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can soak a cabinet in an afternoon. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. Common stainless sizes ride on the truck. For a standard 33 by 22 inch double bowl drop-in, we can often pull the old sink and set a new one the same day.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Rochester, MN

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.
