    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Rochester, MN

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Rochester

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Rochester

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. Pivot nuts leak and drain bodies corrode at the overflow holes, so a sink that will not hold water needs a new pop-up. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Rochester

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Most glass and stone vessel bowls have no overflow hole, so they need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, and a slotted drain in one of those bowls leaks out of the slots right into the counter hole. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Rochester, MN

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.
