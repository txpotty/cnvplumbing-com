    How We Install Showers in Rochester, MN

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Rochester

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. A new pan, a tub conversion or a new valve and trim can change the bathroom without a gut job. We price the options side by side. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Shower Pan Installation in Rochester

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Rochester, MN

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Both prevent scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Rochester

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Rochester

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. When water is getting under the floor or into a ceiling below, we pull the damaged unit, dry what we can and install a standard stall so the bathroom is usable again within a day or two. Custom work can wait. The framing gets inspected first.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Rochester, MN

    Budget showers can still be built right. A one-piece unit with a good valve is a fraction of the cost of tile, and set properly on mortar it holds up for a long time. Spend on the valve. The quote covers everything up front.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Rochester

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. ADA stalls get handheld units. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Rochester

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. A gauge reading comes first.
