    What Goes Into an Ejector System in Rochester, MN

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Rochester

    Residential ejector systems in Rochester serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Rochester, MN

    A dead ejector pump takes every below-grade fixture out of service until it is fixed. Quit using the basement fixtures and the washer if it feeds the pit, then call (612) 712-9569 and we will try to get a plumber and a replacement pump to you that day. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Rochester

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Summer thunderstorms can drop heavy rain in a short time, causing sewer backups in older neighborhoods. Backup power can help.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Rochester, MN

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Rochester, MN

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Filters save impellers.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Rochester

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. The float usually goes first.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Rochester, MN

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. Bigger basins let the pump run longer, fewer times a day, which is easier on the motor and switch, so we size up when the pit serves a laundry or several fixtures. Lids must be gas-tight.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Rochester, MN

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. It keeps the pour on schedule.
