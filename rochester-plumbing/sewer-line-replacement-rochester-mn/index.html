    Sewer Camera Inspection in Rochester

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the depth on the lawn. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Sewer Pipe Materials We Install in Rochester, MN

    PVC covers most of our trenched work. We lay SDR 35 PVC for most buried runs, Schedule 40 where a thicker wall is called for, and fused HDPE for pipe bursting. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and foundation drainage are central to keeping homes dry. Cast iron still has a place inside some buildings.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Rochester

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. Sewer gas and moisture eat away the bottom of the pipe first, so the camera often shows a channel worn through along the invert even when the top still looks solid. Scale snags paper and grease. We swap the outdoor section for plastic pipe. Under-slab cast iron gets a separate look at lining or rerouting.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Rochester, MN

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. If the camera shows a single offset or a cracked section and the rest of the line is round and clean, we dig one small hole at the marked depth and replace that piece with PVC and shielded couplings. Far cheaper than a full run. We only suggest it when the footage backs it up.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Rochester

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. PVC and HDPE do none of that. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Most of our sewer work is single-family homes. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and perimeter drains are central to keeping homes dry. The usual suspects are rooted clay joints, cast iron worn through at the bottom and sections that sank as the soil settled. A new line keeps sewage from coming up through the basement floor drain or a ground floor shower.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Rochester, MN

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. A typical burst takes one working day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.
