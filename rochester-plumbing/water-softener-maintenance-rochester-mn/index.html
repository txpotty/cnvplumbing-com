    Water Softener Maintenance in Rochester, MN, Part by Part

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Rochester, MN

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Rochester

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Rochester

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. Between those, the salt level, brine well and float all get a look. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Rochester, MN

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Emergency Softener Repairs in Rochester, MN

    A softener stuck in backwash can run water to the drain all night, and a cracked brine tank can empty across the floor. Both are same-day problems. First, put the valve in bypass and the rest of the house keeps running, then call (612) 712-9569 and we will get a plumber out.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Rochester, MN

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, so the resin never recharges and hard water flows to every tap.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Rochester, MN

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Rochester

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They change the minerals into tiny crystals that are less likely to stick. The media needs replacing every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.
