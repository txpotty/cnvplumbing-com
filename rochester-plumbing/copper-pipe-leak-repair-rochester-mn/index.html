    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Rochester, MN

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Frozen pipes are a constant winter concern in Minnesota, and deep frost can even reach buried water service lines during severe winters. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Rochester

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. There is less metal to lose. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, with new pipe that is heavier than the original. We check the rest of the run.

    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Rochester, MN

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Repeated leaks change the math. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, and a PEX or Type L copper repipe can cost less over a few years than repeated repairs and drywall patches. You get both numbers.

    Corrosion Leak Repair in Rochester, MN

    Water can be hard on copper over time. Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that stain fixtures and laundry. Thinning walls lead to leaks. We look at corrosion patterns on the pipe we remove, because the location and shape of the pits tell us whether water chemistry, velocity or electrical current is doing the damage. Then we fix that too.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Rochester

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. The meter gives it away. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, then repair it through a small pit or replace the line by pulling new PEX or Type K copper through the old path. We test before backfill.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Rochester, MN

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Crusty fittings are a clue. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. Then we pressure test it.

    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Rochester

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Listen for running water. When those lines leak, you may notice warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water or a meter that turns with every fixture off, and we locate the leak electronically before choosing a spot repair through the slab or a reroute. We price each option.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Rochester, MN

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. It takes about an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, and run a thermal scan on walls where pipe runs if the house has had leaks before. We write up what we find.
