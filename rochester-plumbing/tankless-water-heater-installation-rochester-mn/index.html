    What Goes Into a Tankless Conversion in Rochester, MN

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Commercial hot water loads in Rochester are heavier and more constant than residential. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. We rack units with a common manifold. Service one, keep the others going.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Rochester

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. A far bath may need recirc.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rochester, MN

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Rochester, MN

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. The vent changes as well. We plan both on the quote.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Rochester, MN

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Rochester, MN homes all year long. Rinnai has a wide model range and good parts support, Navien builds in recirculation on many models, Noritz makes compact condensing units that fit tight spaces, and Rheem pairs well with their tank lineup. Each warranty differs. We explain the differences.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion. Since the gas line and vent are already there, most swaps mean matching connections, inspecting the old vent and mounting the new heater. Vent sizes can change by brand. We confirm fit first.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rochester

    Gas tankless units in Rochester are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rochester, MN

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Rochester, MN homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. Panel capacity gets checked up front.
