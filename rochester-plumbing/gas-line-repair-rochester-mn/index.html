    CSST Repair and Bonding in Rochester, MN

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic.     Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Rochester

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Rochester, MN

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Rochester

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Freeze and thaw movement can crack older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Rochester, MN

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Rochester, MN

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Load calculation comes before any pipe. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.
