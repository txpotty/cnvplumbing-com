    Repiping Work We Handle in Rochester, MN

    Slab Leak Repiping in Rochester

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Freeze and thaw movement can shift older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Rochester

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Rochester

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Rochester

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Your policy may pay for water damage even if not for the new pipe, so we take photos of everything we find.

    Commercial Repiping in Rochester, MN

    Commercial buildings in Rochester, MN often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. We use Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX sized from fixture units and installed to commercial code.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Rochester

    Older commercial buildings in Rochester often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. Our crews swap out risers, branches and drains with as little interruption to tenants as the building allows. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. Work is phased by zone, and we coordinate access, keys and noise limits with the property manager.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Rochester, MN

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Rochester

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.
