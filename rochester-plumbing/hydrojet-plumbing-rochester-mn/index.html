    Drain and Sewer Jetting Services in Rochester, MN

    Main Line Jetting in Rochester, MN

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Rochester

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Root Removal Jetting in Rochester

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Rochester, MN

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Rochester, MN

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Summer thunderstorms can drop intense rain in a short time, causing sewer backups in older neighborhoods. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Rochester

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Rochester, MN

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.
