    Why Choose CNV Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Rochester, MN?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: horizontal supports roughly every 32 inches, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. Our crews follow them. Skipping them leads to kinks, sagging lines and fittings that strain, and it can void the pipe warranty the manufacturer would otherwise honor.

    • Fewer Fittings, Fewer joints in the wall cuts the odds of a hidden drip.
    • Faster Repipes, Most houses are done in two to four days.
    • No Pinholes, Plastic pipe cannot pit, so chemistry-driven pinhole leaks stop.

    Citywide Coverage

    Our crews cover Rochester end to end and the towns around it. Older neighborhoods tend to have galvanized or early copper, and newer subdivisions from the late seventies into the nineties are where we find the most polybutylene still in the walls. We know what to look for.

    Experienced Team

    Our plumbers know how to route PEX through cramped attic space, around ductwork and down finished walls. They mark the route on the ceiling and walls before cutting anything, and they pick spots like closets and the backs of cabinets for openings whenever the layout gives them that option. Patches stay small.

    Safety-First Approach

    PEX has to stay out of direct sunlight and away from heat, so we sleeve it in attics and keep it off water heater flues. Most makers also require a metal transition, often 18 inches of copper or a listed connector, between a gas water heater and the first PEX fitting, and we follow those numbers exactly. Hot flues damage PEX.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Rochester, MN

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Rochester, MN

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. An addition gets sized on its own. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Rochester

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.
