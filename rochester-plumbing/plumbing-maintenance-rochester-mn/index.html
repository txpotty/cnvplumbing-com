    Sump Pump Testing in Rochester, MN

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any finished basement. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Hidden Leak Checks in Rochester, MN

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. We shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic and thermal tools to track hidden leaks in walls, under slabs and in buried lines. Mid-century houses usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction relies on PEX and plastic drains. Early finds prevent mold. Usually the fix is minor.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Rochester

    Mid-century houses usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction typically uses PEX and plastic drains. Buried and under-slab water lines can wear from soil movement and hard water until they start leaking where nobody can see it, sometimes for months. Warm floors are a clue. A spinning meter is another. Acoustic and thermal gear pinpoint it.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Rochester, MN

    A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any finished basement.     Scheduled Service for Businesses in Rochester

    Commercial plumbing takes a beating. Large heaters, restrooms, backflow devices and kitchen lines get checked on a set schedule. Health inspectors notice. Each visit comes with a written report for your files.

    Repipe Assessments in Rochester, MN

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. We identify the pipe type, look for signs of failure and tell you whether repiping is urgent or can wait. PEX and copper are the options. Repiping can be done in stages.

    Water Softener Service in Rochester

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Rochester, MN

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Insulating vulnerable pipes, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on in unoccupied homes avoid most freeze damage. During maintenance we look for exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces, hose bibs without vacuum breakers and outdoor lines that need draining, and we fix those before the season that tests them. Insulation is cheap. Burst pipes are not.
