    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Rochester

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently.     Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Rochester

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Rochester

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.
