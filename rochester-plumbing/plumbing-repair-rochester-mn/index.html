    Drain Line Repair in Rochester

    Slow drains that come back a week after snaking usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cast iron that has cracked or a run that sags will hold debris no matter how often it is cleared.     Well Pump Repair in Rochester, MN

    No water at the tap on a well system points to a short list of parts. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. Voltage and amp readings at the control box tell us whether the pump needs to come up. A submersible pull takes two techs and a truck.

    Water Heater Parts Repair in Rochester

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. Rust at the seams is different. That tank is done, and we will say so.

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up every few months. They slip into a cracked joint, then grow into a mat that snags paper and grease.
