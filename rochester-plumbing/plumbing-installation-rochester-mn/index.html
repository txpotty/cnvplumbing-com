    What We Install in Rochester, MN

    PEX Repiping in Rochester

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. Freeze damage is less likely too. Frozen pipes are a constant winter concern in Minnesota, and deep frost can even reach buried water service lines during severe winters. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rochester, MN

    Electric tankless heaters pull serious amperage, often 120 amps or more for a whole-house unit. We confirm your panel and service can handle the draw first, and a licensed electrician joins the job if they need an upgrade. The plumbing side is simpler. Flow and temperature get dialed in so a running dishwasher does not turn the shower cold.

    Water Line Installation and Replacement in Rochester

    Corroded galvanized and brittle polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. We run new lines in PEX or Type L copper, using crimp or expansion fittings on the PEX and soldered joints on the copper. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and foundation drainage are central to keeping homes dry. Buried pipe gets sand bedding and proper depth, so rocks and settling do not cut into it later. Above 80 psi, we add a pressure reducing valve.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Installs in Rochester

    Bradford White and Rheem both make good tanks, and the brand matters less than the fit. We look at hot water demand, the space you have and whether gas or electric is cheaper to run at your house. Then we check the Minnesota code items. Closed systems, the kind with a check valve or PRV at the meter, usually need an expansion tank, which we install alongside the heater.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Rochester

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. A certified tester checks each assembly on install day and the results go to the water provider. Then the clock starts. Retesting is usually yearly, and we keep a reminder list so you do not miss the date.

    Water Line Installation in Rochester

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, so pressure drops a little every year until the shower barely runs. We replace it with PEX or copper and make every connection on the new line leak-free before backfill. Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs showerheads, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. Pipe choice follows the water. That includes the service line from the meter, the branch lines inside and the fixture supplies, all trenched and backfilled to local rules.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Rochester

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. Type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Rochester

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.
