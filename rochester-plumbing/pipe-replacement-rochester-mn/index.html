    What Goes Into a Repipe in Rochester, MN

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Rochester

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Rochester, MN

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Rochester, MN

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Rochester

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around Rochester should test for hardness and iron before choosing a treatment system. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Insulating vulnerable pipes, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on in unoccupied homes avoid most freeze damage. Ice inside a pipe cracks it open. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Rochester

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, then patch every opening before we leave. Clean finish.
