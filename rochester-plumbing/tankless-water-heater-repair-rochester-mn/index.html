    Commercial Tankless Repair in Rochester, MN

    Tankless Flush and Scale Removal in Rochester

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Most tankless installs include isolation valves with purge ports for exactly this job, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. Vinegar works in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Rochester, MN

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rochester, MN

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. When a breaker trips under load, the cause can be a shorted element or a loose, scorched lug, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. We check element resistance and supply voltage. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Rochester

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rochester

    Restaurants and laundromats in Rochester often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. When one unit drops out, the rest carry the load and scale up sooner, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. Downtime costs money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Rochester, MN

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. The gas line is the sneaky one. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, so the unit lights at low flow and fails when two showers run.

    Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Rochester

We also read fan speed from the service menu and compare it to spec, since a fan that cannot reach target RPM will cause flame and exhaust faults on the same unit. We clean the trap and screen, then retest.
