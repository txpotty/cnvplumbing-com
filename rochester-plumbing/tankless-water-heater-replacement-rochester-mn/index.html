    Picking a Tankless for Your Rochester, MN Home

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Rochester

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Rochester, MN

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. The old flue gets capped.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Rochester, MN

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Rochester, MN

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Plan on a full day.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Rochester, MN

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Insulating vulnerable pipes, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on in unoccupied homes prevent most freeze damage. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Rochester, MN

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. You see both quotes.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Rochester

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.
