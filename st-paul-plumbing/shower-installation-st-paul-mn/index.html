    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in St. Paul, MN

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in St. Paul, MN

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Digital Shower Systems in St. Paul, MN

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning.
