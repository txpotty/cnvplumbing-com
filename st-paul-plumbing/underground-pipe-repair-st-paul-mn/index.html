    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in St. Paul

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, so we replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. You will notice the pressure.

    Main Water Line Repair in St. Paul

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Small leaks grow. A spinning meter dial with every fixture off, falling pressure or a wet spot that never dries tell us to locate, dig and splice the line with code-approved fittings. Rusted steel is better replaced.

    Pipe Bursting Services in St. Paul, MN

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in St. Paul

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. One or two holes, not a trench. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, which leaves driveways, patios and mature landscaping in place. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in St. Paul, MN

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the cheapest option. Bursting runs higher. It avoids open trenching and the long-term risk of more leaks on old pipe, while a full trench replacement is sometimes needed when the old route is blocked or the pipe is too far gone. We price all three.
