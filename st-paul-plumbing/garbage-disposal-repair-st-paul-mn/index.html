    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in St. Paul, MN

    After-Hours Disposal Service in St. Paul

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in St. Paul

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most are simple fixes.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in St. Paul, MN

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. It is not a repairable part.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in St. Paul, MN

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in St. Paul, MN

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Coffee grounds and rice cause most of it. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in St. Paul

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. Cabinets are damp places. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in St. Paul, MN

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. That keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Leak Detection and Repair in St. Paul, MN

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.
