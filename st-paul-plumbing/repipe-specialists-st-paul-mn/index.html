    Drain Line Repiping in St. Paul

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside.     PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in St. Paul

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.
