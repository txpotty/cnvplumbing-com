    Pressure above 80 PSI puts constant stress on every joint and valve. Aggressive water can also eat pinholes in copper, which start as a mist and open into a spray.     Overflowing Toilets and Runaway Fixtures in Apple Valley

    A faucet that will not shut off can flood a vanity cabinet in minutes. Older multi-turn stops often seize, so the homeowner cannot close them. We replace the cartridge, fill valve or flapper that failed, and swap the stuck valve for a new quarter-turn stop under the sink. That way you can shut it off yourself next time.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Apple Valley

    A split copper line can put several gallons a minute on the floor. Shut the main first. We match what you have, copper with sweat or press fittings and PEX with crimp rings, so the new piece suits the old system. Summers can be warm and humid, bringing sweating on cold water lines in basements and utility rooms.

    Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated spaces still freeze during cold snaps. Open the faucet before thawing anything. If the line already split, we cut out the damaged length and replace it, then suggest insulation or heat cable for that run.
