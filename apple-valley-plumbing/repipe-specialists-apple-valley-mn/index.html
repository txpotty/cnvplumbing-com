    PEX Repiping in Apple Valley, MN

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Apple Valley, MN

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Apple Valley

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Apple Valley

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Apple Valley

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Glacial clay and sandy soils are both common in Minnesota, and deep frost causes the ground to heave and settle each year. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Apple Valley

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.
