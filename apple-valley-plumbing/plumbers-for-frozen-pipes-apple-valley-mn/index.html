    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Apple Valley, MN

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a spigot with a hose still attached. The hose traps water in the valve.     Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Apple Valley

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawlspaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens. These runs freeze first.     Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Apple Valley

    Drain lines can freeze too, mostly in crawlspaces and outside walls. You will see the backup first. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.

    Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Apple Valley, MN

    Water spraying from a wall or ceiling means a pipe has split. Shut off the main valve now. We replace the split section and test the whole branch, since a freeze often cracks more than one spot.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Apple Valley

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Their brass bodies split easily. We repair or replace it, arrange any required test, and show you how to winterize it.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Apple Valley

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Those get heat cable. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Apple Valley

    Foam pipe insulation costs little and prevents most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. Fit matters. We size the sleeve to the pipe, tape every seam and miter the corners so there are no gaps at elbows and tees, since a single bare fitting is often exactly where a line freezes first.
