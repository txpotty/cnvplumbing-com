    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Apple Valley, MN

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Badger Series Installation in Apple Valley, MN

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Apple Valley

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber.     Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Apple Valley

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.
