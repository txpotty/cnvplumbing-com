    How We Install Showers in Apple Valley, MN

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Apple Valley

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Apple Valley, MN

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Shower Pan Installation in Apple Valley

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Apple Valley

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. An electrician runs the circuit. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Apple Valley

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. Commercial valves with metered or push-button controls keep run times in check, and the drains are sized for heavy use. ADA stalls get handheld units. Work is phased so part of the room stays open.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Apple Valley, MN

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Apple Valley, MN

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. We add solid wood blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare, set the valve within reach of a seat and install a handheld on a slide bar at a height that works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Apple Valley, MN

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. When a pan is cracked or a valve is leaking into the framing, we pull the damaged shower, check the joists and subfloor and install a new unit as quickly as the framing allows. Dry framing first. Then the new shower goes in.
