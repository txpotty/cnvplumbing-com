    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Apple Valley, MN

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Apple Valley

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. First step, kill the breaker to the pump. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and connect it with new fittings and fresh sealant. Usually a morning.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Apple Valley, MN

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Apple Valley, MN

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Some run four or more on a manifold. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, and we schedule the swap around your business hours to keep the water on. Larger tanks need two people.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Apple Valley

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. Small tanks give surprisingly little. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, and target a minimum one-minute run time. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Apple Valley

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. Once the shell leaks, it is done. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, and a pinhole in the shell means replacement, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Apple Valley, MN

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. You get even pressure across fixtures. These systems can run on a smaller tank, though the drive and wiring add to the price. We explain the trade-off.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Apple Valley

    Listen for the switch clicking every few seconds. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, which wears the pump and the switch contacts. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Apple Valley

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Turn the pump breaker off right away. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, and we aim to swap the tank before the pump suffers. Water back tonight.
