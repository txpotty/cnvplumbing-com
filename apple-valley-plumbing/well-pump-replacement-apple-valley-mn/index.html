    What We Replace on Well Systems in Apple Valley, MN

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Apple Valley

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. We replace it with schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe, depending on the depth and the pump weight. Wire gets the same look. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Emergency Well Pump Service in Apple Valley, MN

    Lost water at the house? Call (612) 712-9569 and tell us what the pressure gauge reads. The gauge reading, with the pump running or silent, tells us whether to bring a pump or just a control box. Same-day water is the goal.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Apple Valley

    Sand kills submersibles early. A well that pumps sand will wear impellers down in a few years, so we check for grit in the old pump and talk about setting the new one higher off the bottom or adding a sand separator. Where the pump hangs matters.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Apple Valley, MN

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Apple Valley, MN

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Apple Valley

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Apple Valley

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Apple Valley, MN

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.
