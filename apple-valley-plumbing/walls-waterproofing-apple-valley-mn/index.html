    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Apple Valley, MN

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Apple Valley

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall.     Interior Walls Waterproofing in Apple Valley, MN

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.
