    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Apple Valley

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Freeze and thaw movement can crack older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.
