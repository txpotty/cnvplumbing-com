    Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Apple Valley, MN

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. Replacing the bowl solves both problems.     Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Apple Valley, MN

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Cast iron flanges rust thin. We repair the flange with a stainless ring, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.

    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Apple Valley, MN

    Wall-hung commercial bowls sit on steel carriers inside the wall, and when one cracks we also check that the carrier bolts and the outlet gasket are still sound. Loose carriers crack bowls. We reconnect and test the flushometer before anyone uses the restroom again.

    Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Apple Valley

    Floor-mount commercial bowls take a beating in high-traffic restrooms, and the ones that crack usually do it at the bolt holes from years of being kicked or overtightened. We match the outlet and spud size. We reconnect the flushometer and test it before the restroom reopens.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Apple Valley

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. If the pair is unavailable, a full replacement is the clean answer, and we will price both.

    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Apple Valley

    Frozen pipes are a constant winter concern in Minnesota, and deep frost can even reach buried water service lines during severe winters.
