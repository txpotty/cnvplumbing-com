    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Leak Repair in Apple Valley

    Fixture leaks in Apple Valley, MN usually cost $150 to $400. Slab or underground pipe repairs can run $600 to $5,000 or more, mostly because of access, depth and the amount of concrete or soil that has to come out. We price it after the inspection.

    A dripping faucet can be a DIY job. Leaks in walls or under slabs need real detection gear and a code repair.

    Age and corrosion cause most leaks, along with soil movement and high water pressure. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages still freeze during cold snaps. Scale from hard water adds to the wear.

    Sudden, accidental damage like a burst pipe is often covered. Slow leaks from wear or corrosion are a different story, and your insurer can tell you what applies.

    Repeat pinholes often come from water chemistry, high pressure or thin-walled copper. Pressure gets tested, the regulator gets checked and a repipe comes up if the leaks keep coming.

    Simple fixture repairs take an hour or two. Burst pipe repairs or PEX line fixes can take half a day, and slab leaks may need a day or two for detection and repair when access is tight.
