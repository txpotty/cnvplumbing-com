    Why Polybutylene Fails in Apple Valley

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs aerators, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
