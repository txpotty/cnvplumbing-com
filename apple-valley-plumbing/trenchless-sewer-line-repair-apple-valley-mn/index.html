    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Apple Valley, MN

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Freeze and thaw movement can crack older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Apple Valley

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.
