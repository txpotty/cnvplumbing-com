    One slow sink is usually a local clog. Several slow drains are not.     Trenchless repair fixes the pipe through one or two small access pits. The yard stays mostly intact.     Multiple drains backing up at once is the big one. Watch the tub. Water rising in the shower when the washer drains, a sewage smell near the cleanout, and wet or very green patches over the line tell you the trouble is in the main line, not one fixture. Video makes it certain.
