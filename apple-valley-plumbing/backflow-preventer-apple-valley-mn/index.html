    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Service in Apple Valley, MN

    RPZ assemblies protect against high-hazard cross-connections at restaurants, medical offices, car washes and buildings with chemical feed. They have two independent check valves with a relief port between them that opens and dumps water if either check starts to fail. Plan a place for the water. RPZs from all the major makers are on our list.

    Febco Backflow Preventer Experts in Apple Valley

    Febco assemblies are common on irrigation and commercial lines. We work on the 765 pressure vacuum breaker, the 850 double check and the 825Y and 860 reduced pressure assemblies. Every model has quirks. On a 765, the air inlet poppet and its seat are the usual trouble, and on the 825Y it is often the relief valve diaphragm.
