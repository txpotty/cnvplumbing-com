    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Apple Valley

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Apple Valley, MN

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Apple Valley

    Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around Apple Valley should test for hardness and iron before sizing a treatment system. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
