    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Eden Prairie, MN

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Eden Prairie, MN

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Eden Prairie

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Eden Prairie, MN

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Eden Prairie

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Eden Prairie

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Eden Prairie

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Summers can be warm and humid, bringing condensation on cold water lines in basements and utility rooms. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Eden Prairie, MN

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Eden Prairie, MN

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.
