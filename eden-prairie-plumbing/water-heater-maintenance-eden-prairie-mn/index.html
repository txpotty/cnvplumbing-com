    Electric Element Service in Eden Prairie, MN

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. Good parts stay in.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Eden Prairie

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs aerators, which is why water softeners are so common in the state.     Tank Flush and Inspection in Eden Prairie

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. So does recovery time.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Eden Prairie, MN

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. CO is the risk. The thermocouple or flame sensor gets checked as well.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Eden Prairie

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Eden Prairie, MN

    Small drips become floods.     Anode Rod Checks in Eden Prairie

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.
