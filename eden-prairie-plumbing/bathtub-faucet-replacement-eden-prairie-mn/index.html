    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Eden Prairie

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Eden Prairie

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts clamp to copper with a set screw, and threaded ones screw onto a pipe nipple. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Eden Prairie

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Eden Prairie, MN

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. A handle that snapped off in the open position or a spout that broke loose from its pipe can leave water running or the tub unusable, so we treat those as urgent and send a plumber the same day when we can. Shut off the water first. Leave the tub drain open.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Eden Prairie, MN

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Eden Prairie

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Eden Prairie, MN

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Eden Prairie, MN

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.
