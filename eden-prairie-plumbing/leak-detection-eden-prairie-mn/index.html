    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Eden Prairie, MN

    A brown ring on the ceiling under a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe without opening the whole ceiling.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Eden Prairie, MN

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. We start outside. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, since sprinkler leaks are the first thing most water departments suggest checking.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Eden Prairie, MN

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. We use a camera for these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Eden Prairie, MN

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

    Slab Leak Detection in Eden Prairie

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera on hot water lines.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Eden Prairie

    A soft wet patch near the sidewalk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most. We map the pipe with a line tracer and listen for the leak with a ground microphone, so excavation stays to one small hole instead of a trench across the yard. Repair options follow.

    Commercial Building Leaks in Eden Prairie, MN

    Restaurants, offices and apartment complexes in Eden Prairie pay for every hour a leak keeps running. Sprinkler lines, cooling tower makeup water and long copper mains are common sources, and a leak in any of them can run under a floor or above a drop ceiling for weeks. We isolate and test each zone, then mark leaks for repair. Most tenants never see us.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Eden Prairie

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell come up on most of our calls. All of them mean water. A thermal scan shows the wet patch, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we open one small access hole rather than following the stain around the room.
