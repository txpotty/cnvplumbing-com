    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Eden Prairie

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. A 20-gallon tank might give you five or six gallons. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, aiming for at least one minute of run time per cycle. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Eden Prairie

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Eden Prairie, MN

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Eden Prairie

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Eden Prairie, MN

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Multi-tank setups are common. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, and we schedule the swap around your business hours to keep the water on. Larger tanks need two people.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Eden Prairie, MN

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Steel rusts fast in a damp well house. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.
