    CSST Repair and Bonding in Eden Prairie, MN

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any finished basement.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Eden Prairie, MN

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Eden Prairie, MN. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. We handle the paperwork. Doing it this way keeps your insurance clean and gives you a record of the repair if you sell the house later.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Eden Prairie, MN

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Eden Prairie

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Freeze and thaw movement can crack older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.
