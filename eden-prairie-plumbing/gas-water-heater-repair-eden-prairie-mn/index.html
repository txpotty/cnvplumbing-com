    Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Eden Prairie

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water speeds that up, and so does a tank that has never been flushed. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. Whistling points somewhere else. That usually means a relief valve or a partly closed supply valve.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Eden Prairie

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. A propane tank needs its own orifice and a valve set for roughly 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. We check pressure with a manometer. A bad conversion gets corrected with the right parts.

    Thermostat & Gas Valve Repair in Eden Prairie, MN

    Scalding water at the kitchen tap is a valve problem until proven otherwise. Sensors can drift as they age, and dials sometimes get bumped past the hot mark. Our plumbers in Eden Prairie, MN measure the outlet temperature at the nearest fixture, compare it to the dial and replace the gas control if it is overshooting, because water at 140 degrees can burn a child's skin in a few seconds. Most homes do fine at 120.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Eden Prairie

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. A pilot that will not light at all is more often a clogged orifice or a dead igniter. Main burner problems show up another way. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.
