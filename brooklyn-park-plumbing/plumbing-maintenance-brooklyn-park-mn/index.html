    Water Heater Maintenance in Brooklyn Park

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Scale buildup shortens water heater life and clogs showerheads, which is why water softeners are so common in the state. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Brooklyn Park

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Hard water drops minerals that settle on the bottom of the tank, where they trap heat, waste fuel and make that crackling sound every time the burner or elements fire. We flush it out. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Brooklyn Park

    Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is a smart move during other plumbing work. Buried and under-slab water lines can wear from soil movement and hard water until they start leaking where nobody can see it, sometimes for months. Warm floors are a clue. A spinning meter is another. We use acoustic and thermal tools to find the spot.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Brooklyn Park

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. A small pump circulates descaler. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Brooklyn Park

    Sewer problems hide underground. A camera run from the cleanout shows root intrusion, grease buildup, sags and offset joints long before they cause a backup into the lowest drain in the house. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and foundation drainage are central to keeping homes dry. You see the footage.     Water Softener Service in Brooklyn Park

We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.
