    Propane homes in Brooklyn Park are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane.     Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Brooklyn Park

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.
