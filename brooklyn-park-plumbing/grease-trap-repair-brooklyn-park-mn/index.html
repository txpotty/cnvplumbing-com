    Damaged Baffle Repair in Brooklyn Park

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through.     Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.
