    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Brooklyn Park

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. We set up outside at the cleanout whenever we can, so the jetter hose never has to come through your house or across your floors. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Brooklyn Park

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. A backup closes rooms or stores. We schedule around slow hours and keep video records for property managers.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Low-slope lines clog more. We jet those runs on a schedule that matches the building's use, and we can work after hours so tenants are not dealing with equipment in hallways or restrooms during the day.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around Brooklyn Park should test for hardness and iron before sizing a treatment system. Scouring nozzles remove the scale, and badly scaled cast iron may get a descaling pass too. The difference shows on camera.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.
