    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Brooklyn Park

    Most home plumbing inspections run $150 to $350, depending on the size and age of the house. Camera work costs extra. Adding a sewer camera inspection raises the total, and we tell you the full price before the visit so there is nothing new on the invoice when we finish.

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Noisy pipes are another. Strange sounds from the pipes or the water heater, along with visible moisture or mold near fixtures, usually point to an underlying problem that needs a professional look before it gets worse.

    A general home inspector checks basic function, while a plumber goes deeper with sewer cameras, pressure tests and pipe identification. Many buyers hire both.

    Watching your water meter, looking under sinks and checking for leaks are all things you can do yourself. A plumber adds video, pressure tests and an expert read.

    Plan on one to two hours for most residential inspections. Big homes and long sewers add time.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.

    A standard home inspection takes about one and a half to three hours. Sewer video adds time. Big houses, long sewers or a leak that needs tracking can stretch it, and we give you a time estimate when you schedule.
