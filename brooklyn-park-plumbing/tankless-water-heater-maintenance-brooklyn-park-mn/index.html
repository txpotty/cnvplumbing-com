    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Brooklyn Park

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water.     Tankless Vent Inspection in Brooklyn Park

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit.     Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Brooklyn Park, MN

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.
