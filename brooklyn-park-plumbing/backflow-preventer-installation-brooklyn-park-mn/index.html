    Residential PVBs usually cost $250 to $700 installed. For bigger homes and businesses, double checks and RPZs run $800 to $3,500 and up, depending on size and whether you need an enclosure.

    An RPZ protects high-hazard connections where the contaminant could make someone sick. A double check handles low-hazard lines where the contaminant is not toxic, and the RPZ adds a relief valve that dumps water between its checks, which the double check does not have. The hazard decides it.

    Most residential installs finish in 2 to 4 hours. A full day is possible on commercial jobs with heavy repiping, and we keep the water off for as short a stretch as the work allows. Testing is included in that time.
