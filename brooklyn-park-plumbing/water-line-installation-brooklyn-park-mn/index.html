    Water Line Pressure Testing and Code Compliance in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Every new line gets a hydrostatic pressure test before the trench is closed. The line is capped and pumped above normal working pressure, then watch the gauge for any drop, because a leaking fitting is easy to fix with the pipe exposed and very hard to find once it is buried in Brooklyn Park, MN. A steady needle means a tight line.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Brooklyn Park, MN

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. For long runs, HDPE is the tougher of the two, and we join it with approved compression or heat fusion fittings and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find the line years from now in Brooklyn Park, MN. We keep PEX out of the sun.

    Insulating Water Lines for Minnesota Weather in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Frozen pipes are a constant winter concern in Minnesota, and deep frost can even reach buried water service lines during severe winters. Exposed pipe is the weak point, not the buried run. We wrap the section that comes up into a crawlspace or garage, and in unheated spaces we use closed-cell foam or heat cable rated for water pipe so a cold night does not split the riser at the first fitting in Brooklyn Park, MN. The meter pit gets a look too.

    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Pressure drops when two showers run at once? The old 3/4 inch line could be what is holding you back, especially on houses built decades ago that now feed more bathrooms and irrigation than the original plumber ever planned for in Brooklyn Park, MN. We test static and flowing pressure first.
