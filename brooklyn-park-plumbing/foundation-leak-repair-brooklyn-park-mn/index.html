    Residential Foundation Leak Repair in Brooklyn Park, MN

    For a homeowner in Brooklyn Park, MN, the first question is usually how bad it is. We answer that with a pressure test.     Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Older homes in Brooklyn Park, MN often have soft copper running under the slab. Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around Brooklyn Park should test for hardness and iron before choosing a treatment system. Copper usually pinholes where it rubs on rock. We cut out the damaged length and sweat or press in new Type L copper, then pressure test the whole line before backfilling so you know the repair and the pipe on either side are tight.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Brooklyn Park

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. CNV Plumbing makes new crimp, expansion or solvent-weld joints so the line under your Brooklyn Park foundation is sound again.

    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Brooklyn Park, MN. Tracer gas helps on tiny leaks. A hydrogen and nitrogen blend goes into the empty pipe, and a sniffer picks it up where it rises through the slab, even when the leak is silent.

    Slab Leaks Under Commercial Buildings in Brooklyn Park

    Commercial slabs carry more plumbing and more foot traffic than a house. The risks are the same. Leaks get located and repaired around your hours, and reroute work can happen overnight so customers never notice. Property owners and insurers get a written report.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Brooklyn Park, MN

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out.     Water Line Reroute in Brooklyn Park

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Brooklyn Park

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Radar scanning maps the cables before any cut, and when the leak is too close to a tendon, we reroute the line instead.
