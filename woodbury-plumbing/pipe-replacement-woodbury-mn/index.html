    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Woodbury

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, and keep water on at night whenever we can. Then patching.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Some water is harder on pipe. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, including fittings near the heater. Look at the joints.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Woodbury, MN

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Woodbury

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. It is near the end. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Woodbury

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages still freeze during cold snaps. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Woodbury

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Breaking a slab is messy and costly. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.

    Local plumbers know local homes. Some have attics, some crawlspaces, some slabs. That affects how we route new pipe and how many openings we need, and with homes in Woodbury we often know it before the truck pulls up. We still inspect.
