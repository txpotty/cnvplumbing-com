    Expect a wide range in Woodbury, MN, roughly $2,500 to $8,000 for a typical main line replacement.     Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and foundation drainage are central to keeping homes dry. Expansive clay swells when wet and shrinks when dry, which pushes and pulls on buried pipe. That cycle can crack rigid pipe or separate a joint over time, so we favor flexible PEX or HDPE, sand bedding and a little slack in the line where the ground moves most in Woodbury. Flexible pipe forgives movement.

    Low pressure everywhere in the house points at the main, not one fixture. Rusty water and a wet strip in the lawn are other clues, especially along the path between the meter and the house, and a bill that jumps without a reason usually confirms it. That is how old mains fail.

    A typical residential line takes one to two days, plus time for locates and inspection. Add a day or two for rock or an unusually long run.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the house, and the utility owns the meter and the street side. We confirm who owns what with your provider.

    A sudden drop in pressure is usually the first clue. Brown or discolored water at the faucet is another sign, along with a wet spot in the yard that never dries out and a water bill that climbs while your usage stays the same. Those point to a failing line.
