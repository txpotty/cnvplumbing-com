    After-Hours Septic Backup Calls in Woodbury

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Woodbury

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and a dye test in the drains shows where water comes in or goes out. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Repair Versus Replace in Woodbury, MN

    Most septic systems that fail can be repaired for a fraction of the cost of a new one. You see the repair and the replacement priced next to each other, with our honest pick for a house like yours. A new field is sometimes the right call. Often it is not.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Woodbury

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Woodbury, MN

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. We rebuild or replace linear and rotary aerators, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Woodbury, MN

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. A cracked or missing outlet tee means we dig at the outlet end alone, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.
