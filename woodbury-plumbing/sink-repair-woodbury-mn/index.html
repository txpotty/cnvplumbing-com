    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Woodbury, MN

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Woodbury

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages still freeze during cold snaps. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Most sinks do not need replacing. Swapping a tailpiece or pop-up costs far less than a new sink, and you keep the bowl and faucet you already have. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Woodbury, MN

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Woodbury

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Woodbury

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Woodbury, MN

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.
