    Picking a Tankless for Your Woodbury, MN House

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Woodbury

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Woodbury

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Woodbury

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Summers can be warm and humid, bringing sweating on cold water lines in basements and utility rooms. Showers and laundry run all year, so the gas savings do too.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Woodbury, MN

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages still freeze during cold snaps. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Navien Venting and Install in Woodbury

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Woodbury, MN

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Woodbury, MN

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. We test both.
