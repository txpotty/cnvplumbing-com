    Slab Leak Repiping in Woodbury

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Freeze and thaw movement can shift older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Woodbury

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    Commercial Repiping in Woodbury, MN

    Commercial buildings in Woodbury, MN often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. We use Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX sized from fixture units and installed to commercial code.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Woodbury, MN

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test.     Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Woodbury, MN

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Many insurers ask about it.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Woodbury

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Woodbury, MN

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.
