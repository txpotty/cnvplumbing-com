    How We Set New Sinks in Bloomington, MN

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Bloomington

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. We set the top in silicone, mount the faucet and drain first, and adjust the trap arm to meet the tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Bloomington, MN

    New sinks often get a new disposal. We mount the disposal on a fresh flange with putty, connect the dishwasher knockout so the hose drains through it, and confirm the outlet sits above the trap inlet so it drains properly. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Bloomington

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Bloomington

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.
