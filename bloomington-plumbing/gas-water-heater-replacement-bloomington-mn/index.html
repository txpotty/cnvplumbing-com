    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Bloomington, MN House

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Bloomington, MN

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Bloomington

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Minnesota homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bloomington

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. New flex connectors every time. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Bloomington, MN

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Bloomington

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Bloomington, MN

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. Choosing is simple.
