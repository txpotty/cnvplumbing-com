    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Bloomington

    Commercial Water Line Services in Bloomington, MN

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Bloomington

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Bloomington

    Sometimes the open trench wins. If the old pipe has collapsed or turns hard corners, we dig. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, set the pipe on clean bedding, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Burst Main Line Response in Bloomington, MN

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some insurers will not write a policy on a house that still has it. Worth replacing.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.
