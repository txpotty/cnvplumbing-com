    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Bloomington

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Bloomington

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Insulating vulnerable pipes, draining outdoor faucets and keeping heat on in unoccupied homes avoid most freeze damage. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Bloomington

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Bloomington

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, finishing with smooth patches ready for paint. Clean finish.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Bloomington, MN

    We often leave old galvanized pipe in the wall. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    PEX Repiping in Bloomington

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.
