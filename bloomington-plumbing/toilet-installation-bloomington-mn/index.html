    Types of Toilet Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Smart Toilet Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Bloomington

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks. In many cases we can pull the broken one and set a replacement in the same visit, with a 12 inch rough-in model in round or elongated. Close the stop until then.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Bloomington

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Bloomington, MN

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. That adds up fast. Replacing a single 3.5 gallon toilet in a household of four can save several thousand gallons a year, and some water utilities have offered rebates on high-efficiency models, so it is worth asking yours. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Bloomington, MN

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Bloomington, MN

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor. We bring the flange up with a stacking extender or replace it outright, and if you are moving the toilet on a slab, that means cutting concrete to reroute the drain and patching it afterward. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Bloomington, MN

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. Work gets scheduled before opening or after close, with fixtures staged on site, so each stall is out of service only briefly. We work with your GC on inspections.
