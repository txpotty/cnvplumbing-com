    Water Softener Maintenance Services We Do in Bloomington, MN

    Commercial Softener Service in Bloomington

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so a twin-tank system keeps one tank online while the other recharges. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Resin Rebedding in Bloomington

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Bloomington, MN

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 when they show wear.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Bloomington

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle usually point to worn seals and spacers. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Bloomington, MN

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Bloomington, MN

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. If the tank and valve are in good shape, keeping them makes sense. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Bloomington, MN

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Bloomington

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. Each visit includes a hardness test, logged for your warranty file. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.
