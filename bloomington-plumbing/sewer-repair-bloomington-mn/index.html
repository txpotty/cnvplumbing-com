    Sewer Repair Options for Bloomington, MN Homes

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Bloomington, MN

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Bloomington, MN

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Bloomington

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Bloomington

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. In the meantime, we flag which drains to avoid.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Bloomington, MN

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Bloomington we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Bloomington

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Bloomington

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in Bloomington often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Bloomington

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
