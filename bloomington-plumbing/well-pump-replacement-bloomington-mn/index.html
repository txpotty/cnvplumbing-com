    What We Replace on Well Systems in Bloomington, MN

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Bloomington

    Pump and tank work as a pair. A tank that is too small or has lost its air charge makes the pump start dozens of extra times a day, and every start heats the motor. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. We show you the burned contacts.

    Emergency Well Pump Replacement in Bloomington

    Dry faucets on a well-only property mean no flushing or cooking, so we treat those as same-day calls whenever the crew and the weather allow. The truck carries common 1/2 and 3/4 HP pumps. We also bring splice kits and switches.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. The common failure is losing prime. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Bloomington

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Bloomington, MN

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. The suction line gets a pressure test. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Bloomington, MN

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. The storage tank does the job a battery would. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Well Pump Sizing in Bloomington

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.
