    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Bloomington

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Bloomington, MN

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Bloomington

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Bloomington, MN

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Backups add cost. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Bloomington

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Bloomington

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Bloomington

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

In clay the water stays against the foundation longer, so the pump runs more often and a larger basin helps the motor last. We size for that.
