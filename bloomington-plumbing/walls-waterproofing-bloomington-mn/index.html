    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Bloomington, MN

    Basement Wall Sealing in Bloomington, MN

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Summers can be warm and humid, bringing condensation on cold water lines in basements and utility rooms. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Bloomington, MN

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Bloomington

    Mid-century houses usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction relies on PEX and plastic drains. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Drainage System Integration in Bloomington, MN

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Bloomington

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Bloomington

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Bloomington take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and meet the waterproofing specs in the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Bloomington

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Bloomington, MN

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.
