    Long-Lasting Water Softener Installs in Bloomington, MN

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Bloomington, MN

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. CNV Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Bloomington, MN

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. We remove the old unit, check the loop and drain for problems and install a newer model that fits your current hardness and household size. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Bloomington

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Bloomington

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. Our residential installs start with a test and a few questions about water use, then we pick a grain capacity that regenerates about once a week for your household. That keeps salt use reasonable. One visit covers the full install.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Bloomington

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Bloomington

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Bloomington

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.
