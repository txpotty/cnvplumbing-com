    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Bloomington

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. That number picks the unit. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Bloomington, MN

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Plan on a full day.

    Emergency Tankless Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    A tankless that will not ignite or leaks from the heat exchanger leaves you with no hot water at all. Call (612) 712-9569 any time it quits. We stock common models for same-day replacement. If your exact unit is back-ordered, a temporary repair can sometimes hold until it ships.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Bloomington

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Bloomington, MN

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. We price both.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Bloomington

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.
