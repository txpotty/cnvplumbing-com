    Pipe Bursting in Bloomington, MN

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Commercial sites in Bloomington have longer runs and more laterals. Parking lots sit on top.     Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Bloomington

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Bloomington

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Bloomington, MN

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Bloomington, MN soil.

    Emergency Trenchless Pipe Repair in Bloomington, MN

    A collapsed sewer line stops every drain in the house. That gets fixed fast. Summers can be warm and humid, bringing condensation on cold water lines in basements and utility rooms. First we clear enough to get flow back, then scope it and book the liner or burst as soon as materials and access allow.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Bloomington

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Bloomington yard.
