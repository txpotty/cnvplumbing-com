    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Bloomington

    Prices in Bloomington, MN usually land between $800 and $2,500. A pedestal unit runs cheaper than a cast iron submersible, and the biggest swing comes from whether we have to excavate a new pit and whether you want a battery backup added to the same basin. Every quote is itemized.

    Labor on our sump pump installs is warrantied, typically for one year. Each pump also has a factory warranty, usually one to five years depending on the brand and model, and we write the exact coverage on your invoice so you know which part to call about if something fails. Keep the receipt.

    Once a year is enough for most pumps, ideally before the wet season. A tune-up covers the float, the pit, the check valve and the discharge, and we confirm the discharge outlet outside is open, since a clogged outlet makes the pump run hard against a blocked line. It takes under an hour.

    Liberty Pumps, Zoeller and Wayne are our regulars. All three use cast iron housings and reliable switches, and all three have solid parts support through local supply houses, which matters more than a fancy feature list when something breaks. The pit size picks the model.

    A straight pump replacement generally goes without a permit. New pits, new discharge routes and new circuits may, so we look up the local rules and file for a permit when one is needed.

    Watch for constant running, rapid cycling or grinding noises. Rust stains and a float that sticks are warning signs too, as is water sitting above the switch level without the pump starting. Replace it before the next storm.

    A primary pump does the everyday work. A secondary pump, often on a battery, waits in the same pit, and it kicks on if the primary fails, loses power, or gets overwhelmed by more water than it can move during a heavy storm. Each pump gets its own check valve.
