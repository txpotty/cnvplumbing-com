    Common Sink Repairs in Bloomington, MN

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Bloomington, MN

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Bloomington, MN

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Bloomington, MN

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Bloomington

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and garages still freeze during cold snaps. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Bloomington, MN

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Bloomington, MN

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Bloomington

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Finding Sink Repair Near Me in Bloomington, MN

    Some sink problems look local but are not. A gurgling sink can mean a blocked roof vent, and a sink that backs up when the washer drains points to a clog in the shared line further down. Those get checked before we price anything.
