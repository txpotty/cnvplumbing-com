    Gas Water Heater Installation Cost in Bloomington, MN

    Price starts lowest with an atmospheric vent tank. Power vent and tankless units cost more, and vent changes, gas line upgrades and code corrections on older installs can add to any job. Permits and haul-away are included. You get an operating cost comparison too.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Bloomington

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Bloomington

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. We install their Defender models to the manual, keeping the bottom air intake clear and the clearances right. Thick steel, good parts. Every one goes in to code.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Bloomington

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. Strapping where required. Your household sets the size.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Bloomington, MN

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Bloomington

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. They need an outlet. Termination clearances follow the manual.
