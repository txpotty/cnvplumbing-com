    Residential Backflow Devices in Bloomington

    Homes with irrigation or a fire sprinkler line often need a device to keep that water out of the municipal supply.     Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Bloomington

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It handles low-hazard connections like many fire sprinkler lines, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Bloomington. We leave room for the tester.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Commercial kitchens and chemical injection systems need an RPZ. Because the relief valve can discharge a lot of water, we plan the drain first, then install the assembly above grade with enough room around it for the tester to connect a gauge kit without lying on the floor in Bloomington, MN. We often add an air gap drain.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Bloomington, MN. A spring-loaded air inlet opens when the pressure falls, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible.
