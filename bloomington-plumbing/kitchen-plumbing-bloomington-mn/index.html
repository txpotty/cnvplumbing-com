    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Bloomington

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and either an air gap at the sink or a high loop under the counter, depending on local code.     Kitchen Drain Clog Removal in Bloomington, MN

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line inside the wall.     Grease Trap Repair in Bloomington, MN

    Cracked lids, missing baffles and rusted inlet tees all let grease pass straight through a trap. We inspect each part during cleaning and replace what has failed, in most cases right then. Without a baffle, a trap is only a box. For larger interceptors we arrange pumping first, then go in and inspect the structure, the outlet and the sample port.
