    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Bloomington, MN

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. A gauge on the hose bib tells us your static pressure in a minute, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.

    Toilet Repair and Replacement in Bloomington

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day. Most fixes are a flapper, a fill valve or a new wax ring, and when the bowl is cracked or the toilet is older than the last bathroom remodel, we set a new water-saving model and level it on the flange. Rocking toilets get shimmed and caulked.

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Bloomington

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and perimeter drains are central to keeping homes dry. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Bloomington

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. You can test yours with a five-gallon bucket of water.

    Water Heater Service and Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Tank or tankless, the failures repeat. Tank heaters leak at the base, rumble with sediment or lose their pilot, and tankless units throw error codes when scale builds up in the heat exchanger or the venting is wrong. We flush, descale and repair both types.

    Gas Line Repair and Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas utility first, then call us. We run new black iron or CSST lines for ranges, dryers and grills, then pressure-test the line and check every joint with a leak detector before the appliance gets lit. All gas work is permitted where required.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. Our techs work on Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai heaters, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. Old tanks get hauled off.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Bloomington

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. The truck has cartridges for the big brands, Moen and Delta included, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old angle stops often break when turned.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in Bloomington

    Pricing depends on the job. Simple repairs like a faucet cartridge or toilet rebuild tend to cost $150 to $350, a water heater installation is often $1,200 to $3,500, and slab leak repairs or full repipes can reach several thousand dollars. The exact number comes after the diagnosis.

    Soft water and normal pressure prevent a lot of what we see. Keep grease out of the drains and test pressure once a year, since anything over 80 psi wears out supply hoses and fixtures faster than people expect. An annual check finds small issues early.

    Our plumbers install and service Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Rheem, Bradford White and Navien, among others. We will tell you which ones hold up in your water, since cartridges in some brands handle hard water better than others. A few cost more up front and save on repairs later.

    A slab leak is a break in a water line running under a concrete foundation. Basements are the norm, so drain tile, sump pumps and perimeter drains are central to keeping homes dry. Warm spots on the floor, a sudden jump in the water bill or the sound of water running with every fixture off are the usual signs. It will not fix itself.

    All the major ones. Moen, Kohler, Delta and American Standard cover most of the fixtures we see, and for water heaters we work on Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai tanks and tankless units every week. We can source parts for less common brands.

    One to two hours covers many repairs. Plan on half a day for a water heater swap, and a whole-house repipe runs a few days, with the water turned back on each evening so the family can shower and cook. We tell you up front.

    Some jobs are fine to DIY. Swapping a flapper or clearing a sink trap is well within reach, but gas lines, water heaters and anything inside a wall can turn into a flood, a fire risk or a failed inspection if the work is off. For those, hire a licensed plumber.

    Every repair comes with a written parts and labor warranty. New fixtures and water heaters also carry manufacturer coverage, and we register it for you, which matters because some water heater makers shorten the tank warranty if the unit is never registered. Save the serial number.
