    Pressure Switch Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Bloomington, MN

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Bloomington

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Bloomington, MN

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, including fast cycling and water at the Schrader valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Bloomington, MN

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, which helps the tank resist rust from the inside, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Bloomington, MN

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Some run four or more on a manifold. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, and we schedule the swap around your business hours to keep the water on. Larger tanks need two people.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Bloomington

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Bloomington

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. Once the shell leaks, it is done. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, so a torn bladder or rusted seam means a new tank, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.
