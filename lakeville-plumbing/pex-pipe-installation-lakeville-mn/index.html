    Copper to PEX Conversions in Lakeville

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around Lakeville should test for hardness and iron before sizing a treatment system. A switch to PEX ends it. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Lakeville, MN

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Lakeville, MN

    Gray polybutylene was installed widely from the late seventies into the mid nineties.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Lakeville

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring.
