    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Lakeville

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard.     Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Lakeville

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Lakeville, MN

    Besides repairs, CNV Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, for example when a restaurant adds fixtures or a gym adds showers.     Underground Leak Detection in Lakeville, MN

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, a method that catches leaks too quiet for acoustic gear. Glacial clay and sandy soils are both common in Minnesota, and deep frost causes the ground to heave and settle each year. Once the spot is marked, you get a written repair price before we dig.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Lakeville

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. We confirm it in about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and continued movement tells us the leak is in the buried line. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Lakeville

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Main Water Line Repair in Lakeville
