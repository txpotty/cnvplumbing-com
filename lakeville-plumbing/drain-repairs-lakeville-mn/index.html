    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Glacial clay and sandy soils are both common in Minnesota, and deep frost causes the ground to heave and settle each year. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, and we locate the bad section on camera before replacing it with wrapped perforated pipe in washed stone. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Lakeville

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Undersized drains spill over. We fix primers that keep those seals full.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Lakeville

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and each one shows up clearly on a camera or when we pull the trap.     Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.
