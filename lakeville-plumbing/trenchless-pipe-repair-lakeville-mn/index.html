    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Lakeville

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Freeze and thaw movement can crack older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints.     Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Lakeville

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner.
