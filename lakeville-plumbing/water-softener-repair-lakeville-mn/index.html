    Valve, Resin and Brine Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Control Valve Rebuild in Lakeville

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Lakeville

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Lakeville, MN

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Lakeville

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. CNV Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Lakeville, MN

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. CNV Plumbing repairs these units in Lakeville, MN. Media lasts a few years.

    Resin Bed Problems in Lakeville

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Lakeville

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.
