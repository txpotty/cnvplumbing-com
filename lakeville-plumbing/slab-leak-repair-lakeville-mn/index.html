    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Lakeville

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. New line goes overhead. For older Lakeville homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Lakeville, MN

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. We open the floor over the leak, dig to the pipe, replace the bad section and pressure test it, then compact the trench and patch the concrete so your flooring contractor can finish the surface.

    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Commercial slabs in Lakeville carry longer pipe runs and more branches. Locating takes longer.     Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Lakeville

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Lakeville home.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.
