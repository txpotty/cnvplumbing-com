    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in Lakeville

    Pricing depends on the job. Simple repairs like a faucet cartridge or toilet rebuild tend to cost $150 to $350, a water heater installation is often $1,200 to $3,500, and slab leak repairs or full repipes can reach several thousand dollars. You get an exact quote after we diagnose.

    Every one to two years works for most homes in Lakeville, MN. The inspection usually runs about an hour, and we check pressure, look for slow leaks at shutoffs and supply lines, test the water heater relief valve and look for corrosion on exposed pipe. Problems found early stay cheap.

    Gurgling is the early one. Slow drains, sewer smells and water backing up in a shower are the others, and when more than one fixture acts up at the same time, the clog is usually in the main line rather than a single trap. Main line clogs rarely clear on their own.

    Watch for drips that will not stop, slow drains, banging or whistling pipes, a sudden drop in pressure, discolored water and wet spots with no obvious source. Any one of these points to a problem behind the wall or under the floor. Do not wait on them.

    Tightening a loose connection or plunging a toilet is fine. We usually advise against complex repairs on your own, because a bad sweat joint, a cross-threaded fitting or a gas connection done wrong can cost far more than the original repair would have. Gas work in particular should go to a licensed plumber.

    Two leaks in a year is the tipping point for most homes. Polybutylene or galvanized supply pipe pushes it toward a repipe, too, since patching one spot on a failing system just moves the next leak a few feet down the line. You get both prices side by side.

    Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. A softener swaps those minerals out through ion exchange, and the difference shows up as scale in water heaters, white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and soap that will not lather well. Soft water is easier on fixtures.

    Most repairs take one to three hours. Fixing a leaky pipe or clearing a clog often takes less than two hours, while water heater installs take most of a day and full repipes can stretch over several days depending on the size of the house and access. We give you a timeline up front.
