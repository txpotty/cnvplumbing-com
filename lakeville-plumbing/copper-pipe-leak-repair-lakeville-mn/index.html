    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust.     Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Lakeville

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. More pinholes often follow.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Lakeville, MN

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, so we repair leaking hot lines and can add a timer or adjust the pump to cut down on wear. Less run time, less wear.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Lakeville

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. Thin walls wear through first. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, replacing the damaged length with Type L copper so the repair has a thicker wall than the pipe it replaced. We check the rest of the run.
