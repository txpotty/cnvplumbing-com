    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Lakeville

    Residential jetting in Lakeville usually costs $350 to $800. Commercial work is priced by the hour. The final number depends on how long the line is, how easy it is to reach and how much buildup is inside.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. Untrained use can hurt people and pipes.

    Clogs that keep coming back in the same drain. That is the main one. Slow drains around the house, gurgling and odors suggest buildup that a cable will not fully remove.

    Buildup responds best. Grease, scale and sludge. Jetting also handles hair, soap and root hairs, because it cleans the full circumference of the pipe.

    Frequent clogs in the same drain. Gurgling is another clue. Odors, gurgling and multiple slow drains usually point to buildup that needs more than a cable.
