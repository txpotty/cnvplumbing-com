    Why Polybutylene Fails in St. Cloud

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
