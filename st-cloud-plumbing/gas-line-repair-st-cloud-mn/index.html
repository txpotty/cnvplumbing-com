    Propane Gas Line Service in St. Cloud, MN

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in St. Cloud

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in St. Cloud

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in St. Cloud, MN

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in St. Cloud, MN. Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. We handle the paperwork. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in St. Cloud, MN

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in St. Cloud

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from St. Cloud building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspectors often come within the week. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    CSST Repair in St. Cloud

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. We check bonding on every CSST job. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in St. Cloud

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Freeze and thaw movement can shift older sewer laterals, and tree roots often find their way into the joints. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.
