    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in St. Cloud

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in St. Cloud, MN

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around St. Cloud should test for hardness and iron before sizing a treatment system. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in St. Cloud, MN

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down.     Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in St. Cloud, MN

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in St. Cloud

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Main Water Line Replacement in St. Cloud, MN

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patching that kind of pipe tends to buy months, not years.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in St. Cloud

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is a smart move during other plumbing work. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.
