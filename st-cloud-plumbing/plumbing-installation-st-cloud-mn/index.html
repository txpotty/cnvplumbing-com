    Water Heater Replacement in St. Cloud

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment.     Water Heater Installation in St. Cloud

    Tank or tankless, gas or electric, the first question is how many people shower in the morning. We size the heater to that peak demand and your fuel source, then check the venting and gas supply before quoting. Gas units get the most attention.     Natural Gas and Propane Lines in St. Cloud, MN

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Water Line Installation and Replacement in St. Cloud

    Corroded galvanized and brittle polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. New lines go in as PEX or Type L copper, with crimp or expansion fittings on the plastic and solder on the copper. Glacial clay and sandy soils are both common in Minnesota, and deep frost causes the ground to heave and settle each year. We bed buried pipe in sand at the right depth so stones and settling soil cannot chafe it over time. Above 80 psi, we add a pressure reducing valve.

    Gas Line Installation in St. Cloud, MN

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. We size the pipe from that, pick black iron or CSST, and trench where the line goes underground. Each joint is checked with leak solution. Then the whole line holds pressure on a gauge before the inspector sees it.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in St. Cloud, MN

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in St. Cloud

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in St. Cloud, MN

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Most gas units need upsized gas piping and a new vent run in stainless or PVC. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. Water heaters and boilers run long hours through the heating season, which adds wear compared to milder climates. Every unit gets isolation valves so it can be flushed.
