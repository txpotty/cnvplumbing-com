    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in St. Cloud, MN

    Older homes in St. Cloud often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. Then we locate it. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in St. Cloud

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in St. Cloud, MN

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Records matter for inspectors. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in St. Cloud, MN

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Water Heater Inspections in St. Cloud

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in St. Cloud
