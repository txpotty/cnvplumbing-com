    What Our Minneapolis, MN Sewer Line Crews Handle

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Minneapolis, MN

    A waterproof camera shows the pipe condition. Roots and corrosion too. The video shows what is wrong and where, so we can recommend the most cost-effective fix.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Minneapolis, MN

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. Waste catches on them. After cutting the mass with a root saw, we jet the line to clear root hairs and leave the pipe clean, then show each entry point on camera.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Minneapolis

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. Roots seek out moisture and nutrients through tiny gaps, and we use root cutters and jetting to clear them from the line, then explain options to keep them from coming back.

    Restaurant and Shop Sewer Lines in Minneapolis, MN

    Commercial lines clog in their own ways. Debris in shops. We set up cleaning to fit the type of business, using larger equipment for bigger lines and heavier waste, working quickly to limit downtime.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Minneapolis

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. Grease, paper and scale. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Minneapolis, MN

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Minneapolis

    A camera shows the line in real time. Problems become obvious. We look for low spots where water pools, offset joints, cracks, roots and corrosion, and a locator in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the surface, with a copy of the video for you.

    Clearing Household Laterals in Minneapolis

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots lead the list. Wipes, grease and roots cause most household clogs, and a camera tells us which method will work best, so the fix lasts.
