    Toilet Brands We Replace in Minneapolis

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score. Parts availability counts too. A toilet with proprietary parts can turn a simple repair into a special order later.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Minneapolis

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Minneapolis, MN

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Minneapolis, MN

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. You get our honest take on which models to skip. Rough-in and bowl shape decide what fits, and we check both before you buy.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Minneapolis

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Minneapolis

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. The drain location stays the same. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.
