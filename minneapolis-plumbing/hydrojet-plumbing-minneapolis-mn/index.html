    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Minneapolis

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Minneapolis, MN

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Private wells are widespread, so homeowners around Minneapolis should test for hardness and iron before choosing a treatment system. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Minneapolis

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Minneapolis

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. Our on-call trucks carry jetters. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.

    Root Removal Jetting in Minneapolis

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Minneapolis

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Minneapolis, MN
