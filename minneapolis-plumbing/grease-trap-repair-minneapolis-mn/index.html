    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Minneapolis

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Minneapolis

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. Repeat clogs nearly always have a cause.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Minneapolis

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Minneapolis

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.
