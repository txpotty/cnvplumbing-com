    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Minneapolis

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Minneapolis

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. The bottom of a cast iron pipe goes first, and the camera will show a trench rusted through even when the top looks fine. Rough scale catches everything.
