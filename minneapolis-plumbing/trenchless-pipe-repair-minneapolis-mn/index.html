    Why Choose CNV Plumbing for Trenchless Pipe Repair in Minneapolis, MN?

    Traditional pipe replacement can mean digging up concrete, pavers and landscaping that took years to establish. Trenchless repair avoids most of that and is usually a one-day job.

    • New Pipe Inside, The liner forms a new, rust-proof pipe inside your existing sewer.
    • Root Resistant, New liners and HDPE pipe resist roots and corrosion better than the clay or cast iron they replace.
    • Seamless Pipe, Lined and burst pipes have no joints for roots to get into.

    Citywide Coverage

    From tight urban lots to larger properties, we bring trenchless repair to every part of Minneapolis. We pick pit spots carefully. Access pits get placed in lawn or gravel rather than under prized landscaping or new concrete, and we restore them after.

    Safety-First Approach

    Trenchless repair disturbs far less soil than a trench. Less waste goes to landfill. With the old pipe left as the host, there is little debris, and leftover resin is disposed of per the manufacturer's rules.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Minneapolis

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Minneapolis

    Older Minneapolis homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Commercial Trenchless Solutions in Minneapolis

    Commercial sites in Minneapolis have longer runs and more laterals. Parking lots sit on top. Schools, offices and stores keep lots and walkways open because we work from cleanouts and manholes, cutting only small pits. We plan work for slow periods.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Minneapolis, MN

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. That's not the whole bill. After adding drive, patio, landscaping and sprinkler repairs to the trench bid, trenchless usually wins on price and speed. We compare both with real numbers for your home.

    Sectional Point Repair in Minneapolis

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Minneapolis sewer lines.

    Residential Trenchless Pipe Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Homeowners in Minneapolis, MN mostly call about sewer laterals. Roots cause most of it. Drain lines under slabs and water service lines can be done too, always with the aim of fixing the pipe and leaving the yard alone. CNV Plumbing quotes each option in writing.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Minneapolis, MN

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Lining comes next. If a liner is a good fit, we book the repair quickly, keep the line flowing where possible in the meantime and tell you what to avoid flushing until the work is complete.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. CNV Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.
