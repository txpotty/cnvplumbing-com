    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Minneapolis, MN: The Details

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. Drain placement is planned early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Minneapolis

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Minneapolis

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Minneapolis, MN

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. Power is a separate question. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Minneapolis, MN

    A drop-in swap costs less than an undermount on stone. Each extra, like a new disposal, a moved drain, a lowered wall stub or replacing galvanized stubs, adds labor and shows up as its own line on the quote. Plumbing is priced apart from the counter.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Minneapolis, MN

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Minneapolis

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.
