    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Minneapolis

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster. Pumping and hauling are both on us. Our vacuum trucks pull the full contents of the trap into a sealed tank, and the load gets logged with gallons and destination. No barrels left behind.

    Residential Grease Trap Cleaning in Minneapolis, MN

    Homes rarely have a grease trap, though a few large custom kitchens and some home-based catering setups in Minneapolis, MN put a small unit under the sink. Most are 10 to 20 gallon boxes. We pull the lid, scoop out the grease and solids by hand, clean the baffle and reset the gasket so it does not leak or smell. About thirty minutes.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Minneapolis

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. Both get checked every visit. Gaskets are cheap to swap, but a broken vent can push sewer gas into the kitchen and slow the drain too, so it is worth chasing. Most smells get fixed that visit.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Minneapolis, MN

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.

    Commercial Grease Trap Pumping in Minneapolis

    Fryer-heavy menus load a trap fastest. Two weeks is enough for a wings place to fill a 50-gallon trap. The trucks have the hose length for traps behind walk-ins or in basements, plus tank room for interceptors over 1,000 gallons. Each pump-out goes to the bottom.

    Grease Line Jetting in Minneapolis

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. We run a camera after to confirm.
