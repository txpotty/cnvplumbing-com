    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Minneapolis, MN

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Minneapolis

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. We install them with the correct inside parts kit for the bowl's rated volume, set the control stop so the flush is strong without splashing, and keep rebuild kits on the truck for later service. Sensor versions mount the same way. Battery models need no wiring.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Minneapolis, MN

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. American Standard, Kohler, Zurn and Sloan all make reliable urinals, and we steer customers toward models whose flush valves and cartridges are stocked at local supply houses so a repair never waits on shipping. Each of those brands is routine for us. Spec sheets come first.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Minneapolis

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Minneapolis

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Corroded hanger bolts and a flush valve seized to the spud mean we come prepared to cut, and we protect the wall tile while we do it so the new fixture does not sit against broken edges. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Minneapolis, MN

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Low-flow and waterless units leave mineral buildup in the line, so proper slope and a cleanout matter. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.
