    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Minneapolis, MN

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Frozen pipes are a constant winter concern in Minnesota, and deep frost can even reach buried water service lines during severe winters. During maintenance we look for exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces, hose bibs without vacuum breakers and outdoor lines that need draining, and we fix those before the season that tests them. Insulation is cheap. Burst pipes are not.

    Sump Pump Testing in Minneapolis, MN

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any finished basement. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Minneapolis

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Cold showers are avoidable. A yearly tune-up catches the warning signs.

    Softener Upkeep in Minneapolis, MN

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. Bypass and drain get tested. We measure hardness after.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Minneapolis

    Sewer problems hide underground. A camera run from the cleanout shows root intrusion, grease buildup, sags and offset joints long before they cause a backup into the lowest drain in the house. Glacial clay and sandy soils are both common in Minnesota, and deep frost causes the ground to heave and settle each year. You see the footage. Early finds often mean a small repair rather than a whole new line.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Minneapolis

    Mid-century houses usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction relies on PEX and plastic drains. A spinning meter is another. Acoustic and thermal gear pinpoint it.
