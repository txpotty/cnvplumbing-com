    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Minneapolis

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. It also tolerates freezing better. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.

    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Minneapolis

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Frozen pipes are a constant winter concern in Minnesota, and deep frost can even reach buried water service lines during severe winters. If a line bursts in your house, close the main shutoff, then call us so we can stop the leak, make a temporary repair and restore water. A full replacement comes next. Waiting for the next burst is the most expensive way to deal with polybutylene.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Minneapolis

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Water across much of Minnesota is hard, and many supplies also carry iron or manganese that discolor fixtures and laundry. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. Buried poly service lines split, run up bills and saturate the yard. Utilities get marked first. Then we trench the route, install new PEX or copper service pipe and connect it to a new main shutoff at the house.

    Buying or selling a house built between about 1978 and 1995 in Minneapolis, MN? Check for poly. We look at the pipe at the water heater, under sinks, at the main and in the attic, read any stamps and give you a written finding and a replacement estimate. It helps in negotiations.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Minneapolis

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.
