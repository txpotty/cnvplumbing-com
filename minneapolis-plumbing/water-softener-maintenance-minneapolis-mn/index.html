    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Minneapolis

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Resin Rebedding in Minneapolis

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Minneapolis, MN

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Emergency Softener Repairs in Minneapolis, MN

    A softener stuck in backwash can run water to the drain all night, and a cracked brine tank can empty across the floor. Either one is worth a same-day call. First, put the valve in bypass and the rest of the house keeps running, then call (612) 712-9569 and we will get a plumber out.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Minneapolis, MN

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, so the resin never recharges and hard water flows to every tap.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Minneapolis, MN

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out.
