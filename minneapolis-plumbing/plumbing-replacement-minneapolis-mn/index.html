    Well Pump and Pressure Tank Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Rural properties near Minneapolis, MN depend on the well pump for every drop, so a failure is an emergency. Common signs include pressure that rises and falls, sputtering faucets, a pump that runs constantly or a breaker that keeps tripping, and any of those means the pump, switch or tank is close to giving out. A failing pump seldom gets better.

    Pipe Replacement and Repiping in Minneapolis

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipe in Minneapolis leads to rusty water and expensive leaks. Failing sections or the whole house get new PEX or copper, routed to keep openings small and pressure tested long enough to catch any slow drip. Patching is included.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    A dead sump pump during a storm floods a basement fast, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Minneapolis, MN, we replace sump pumps with cast iron or thermoplastic models sized for your inflow, add a check valve, and recommend a battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and the pressure switch setting. Both get tested before we go.

    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Minneapolis

    Rural homes around Minneapolis that run on a well go dry the moment the pump stops. The old pump comes up, wire and drop pipe get checked, and the new pump goes in at the right depth, often with a new tank and switch too. Water is usually back that day.

    Gas Line Replacement in Minneapolis

    Old black iron or corroded gas lines in Minneapolis homes can leak at threaded joints, and a gas leak is not something to wait on. Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas company from outside, and once it is safe we replace the bad section or the full run, test it and relight everything. Testing comes before relight.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Switching to tankless when the old tank fails is common in Minneapolis, MN, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually needs to be upsized, and the vent changes from metal B-vent to stainless or PVC. We price both up front.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Minneapolis, MN

    Homes in Minneapolis, MN with galvanized steel or polybutylene tend to show the same symptoms: low pressure, rusty water and leaks that keep coming back. A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture with PEX or copper, and in most homes it takes two to four days with water back on each evening whenever possible. We patch every opening.
