    Drain Repair Options for Minneapolis, MN Homes

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. We test it before closing up.

    Drain Vent Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Many vent fixes take under an hour.

    Preventive Drain Maintenance in Minneapolis

    Scheduled cleaning keeps buildup from turning into a repair. Most homes need it yearly. For older homes in Minneapolis with cast iron or a history of kitchen clogs, a yearly jetting and camera check catches small defects before they back up. Hard water lines benefit most.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Minneapolis

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then run a camera if the clog returns within a few weeks or sits more than a few feet past the trap, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. That decides the repair.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Minneapolis

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Drain seals wear out. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. Any access hole is kept small and neat.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Minneapolis

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Minneapolis

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Hard water makes it worse. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Figure on about an hour for a typical fix.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. Fragile pipe gets checked before jetting.
