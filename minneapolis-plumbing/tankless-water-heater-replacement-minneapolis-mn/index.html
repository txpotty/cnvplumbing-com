    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Minneapolis

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house.     Leaking Tankless Replacement in Minneapolis

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Minneapolis, MN

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. Installs can happen after closing.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Minneapolis

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Minneapolis

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Minneapolis, MN

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. We test both.
