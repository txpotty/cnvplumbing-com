    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Minneapolis, MN Home

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Minneapolis

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. Leaky lids let sewer gas into the basement.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Minneapolis

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Minneapolis

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. Then we find out why it failed.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Minneapolis, MN

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. Test them each spring.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Minneapolis

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Minneapolis

    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise groundwater fast in Minnesota, sending water into basements and overloading sump pumps. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Minneapolis

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. We tell you which before we sell you anything.
