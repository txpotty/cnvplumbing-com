    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most.     Electric Water Heater Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. You get more spots to put it.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Minneapolis

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Minneapolis

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Minneapolis

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. We follow them exactly. Your warranty stays intact.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Minneapolis

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Carbon monoxide is the risk. All work follows local code.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Minneapolis

    Water heater installation in Minneapolis, MN runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. Type, capacity and fuel set most of the price, and any gas line, venting or electrical upgrades add to it. Tankless sits at the high end. You get a written quote after we see the space.

    Tank heaters usually last 10 to 15 years, and tankless units can go 20 or more. Hard water and skipped maintenance shorten that, while yearly flushing and anode checks stretch it a good deal past the average. Flushing helps most.

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are our most common installs. We carry gas and electric models in several sizes and efficiency levels, and we can order most others within a day or two. You pick with our help.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. We give you a timeline first.

    For two to four people, a 40 or 50 gallon tank usually does the job. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    It looks simple but involves gas, electrical and plumbing connections, plus a vent that has to carry exhaust out of the house every time the burner fires. A mistake can mean leaks, CO exposure or a fire. Hire a licensed plumber instead.

    Manufacturer warranties on the tank and parts usually run anywhere from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model and the brand you choose. CNV Plumbing adds a labor warranty on our work. Ask us for exact terms.
