    Outside Wall Waterproofing in Minneapolis, MN

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Minneapolis

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. Bad sections get cut out and replaced, and a run with repeated pinholes gets repiped rather than patched. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Minneapolis

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Minneapolis

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.
