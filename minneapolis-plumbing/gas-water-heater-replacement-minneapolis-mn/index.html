    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Minneapolis

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    No Hot Water Calls in Minneapolis

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. We help you pick.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Minneapolis, MN

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes last longest. We price it against how long you plan to stay.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Minneapolis, MN

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Minneapolis, MN

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. Then we test draft with the burner on.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Minneapolis

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Minnesota plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.
