    What Goes Into a Repipe in Minneapolis, MN

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Minneapolis, MN

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, so we avoid tearing open walls just to pull out dead pipe. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Minneapolis

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, and usually costs less than repairing in place. Fast too.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Shut off the main valve first. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and we can usually get there, stop the leak and replace the failed section the same day. Drying comes next.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Minneapolis

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Water lines are buried deep for that reason, but pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated spaces still freeze during cold snaps. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Minneapolis

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Minneapolis, MN

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.
