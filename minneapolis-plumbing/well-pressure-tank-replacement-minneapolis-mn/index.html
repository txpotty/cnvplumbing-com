    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Minneapolis

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. Once the shell leaks, it is done. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, and a pinhole in the shell means replacement, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Minneapolis, MN

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Minneapolis, MN

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. Common sizes are easy for us to get. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Minneapolis

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Minneapolis, MN

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Minneapolis

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, which means draining it to restore the air. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Minneapolis

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.
