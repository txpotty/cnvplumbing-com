    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Minneapolis

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Minneapolis

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Minneapolis, MN

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Minneapolis, MN

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Most no-heat calls come down to a failed igniter or an exchanger so scaled that the high-limit switch keeps tripping. We usually fix it on the first trip, since igniters and descaling gear ride along.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Minneapolis, MN

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. So we schedule the work before opening or after close.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Minneapolis

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. Spent media gets replaced. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Minneapolis, MN

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.
