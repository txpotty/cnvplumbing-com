    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Minneapolis

    A simple solder repair usually runs $200 to $500. Hidden pipe costs more. A slab leak that needs specialized detection and repair could range from $800 to $3,000 or more, with reroutes at the top of that range. The written quote follows diagnosis.

    If you have had repeated leaks, it is worth pricing. PEX resists that corrosion. PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and goes in quickly with fewer wall openings, so a PEX repipe often beats years of patching. We price both options.

    An accessible pinhole or a single joint takes one to four hours. Slab leaks take longer. Leaks under a slab or a repipe can take a full day or more, depending on access, whether the line is rerouted and how much copper needs replacing. The quote includes a time estimate.

    Insurance often covers sudden water damage. Not the pipe itself. The cost of repairing old or corroded copper is usually yours, but the resulting damage to the house may be claimable. Check with your agent. We provide documentation.

    Keep water pressure under about 80 psi. A pressure valve does it. Treating hard or aggressive water, insulating pipe in cold spots and limiting how long a recirculation pump runs all reduce the wear that leads to pinholes and failed joints. Find the main valve now.

    Minor repairs typically do not. Bigger changes might. Significant repiping, relocating lines or work on the main water service in Minneapolis may need a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. The paperwork is on us.

    We typically use Type L copper. It has a thicker wall. Type L lasts longer than the Type M found in many older homes, while press fittings or a PEX transition get used when a torch is a bad idea. All materials are potable-rated.

    Clamps and epoxy putty can stop a drip for a day. They will not last. A proper repair means cutting out the thin copper and soldering or pressing in new pipe, since a failed patch at full pressure can soak a floor in minutes. Use it only until a plumber arrives.
