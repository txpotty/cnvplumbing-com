    Water Heater Problems We Fix in Minneapolis, MN

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Minneapolis

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. Mineral buildup on the heat exchanger triggers overheating codes and shutdowns. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    Pilot Repair in Minneapolis

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Minneapolis

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. The tank gets drained and refilled several times to break up scale, and the drain valve gets cleared. A flush brings back lost capacity. Homes on hard water benefit from a yearly flush.

    No Hot Water Diagnostics in Minneapolis

    Diagnosis starts with fuel. We confirm the gas is on, the breaker is set and the pilot is lit. Then the thermostat, element or gas valve gets tested one by one, and we recommend the least expensive repair that will last. Simple checks first.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. First we check the thermostat and the water pressure. On homes with a pressure regulator, a missing expansion tank is often the real problem. Loose fittings get tightened or replaced.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Minneapolis

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. Lukewarm water that runs out fast usually means the lower element. We test resistance with a meter. Elements and thermostats are on the truck, so the fix is usually done the same visit.

    Electric Tank Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Scale from hard water buries the lower element and burns it out. We test resistance and voltage, replace the failed part and reset the high limit, then watch a full heating cycle before we call it done. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Minneapolis, MN

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow flame tips or soot at the draft hood mean the burner is not getting enough air to burn cleanly. We clean the burner and check draft with a mirror or a smoke test. Backdrafting is dangerous.
